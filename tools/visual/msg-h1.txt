Journey home: the hero and every band keep the header's edge (16px to 1023, 32px from 1024)

The same owner's rule on `/`. The hero (.kp-hero__inner) and every band (.kp-band__inner, which is every section on
the home page) step to 24px at 768. Against the header's and the pages' 16 / 32, that put `/` 8px inside the
header from 768 and 8px outside it from 1024 (G5's tiles 017/018 and 047/048).

The fix is scoped to the journey, by the shell's server-rendered mark: `:root:has(#kp-journey-shell)`. The span is in
the HTML before any script runs, so the first paint already has these values and nothing moves on hydration.
Classic viewers are served what they were (VODACOM-PLAN §0i; qa:classic-shell-parity), and the classic home keeps
its 24. A browser without :has() keeps 24, which is the classic look and not a break. The global alternative would
have moved the classic home for every player now, against the plan's rule that nothing changes for players before
the launch.
