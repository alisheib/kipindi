Journey Wallet: no Deposit for a reader on a break — Withdraw alone, full width

Found while planning the edge-scenario capture (a player on a cooling-off break). The journey header withholds
"+ Weka pesa" during a break (S4), and /wallet stops inviting a deposit (depositOpen, 2026-10-06), because
/wallet/deposit refuses one and shows the break's sentence. But the Wallet the balance capsule opens still offered
Weka pesa beside Toa pesa: a door to an action the next screen refuses.

- wallet-sheet.tsx: a new onBreak prop. When it is set, the Deposit column is not rendered and the pair becomes one
  column (.kp-wsheet__pair--one), so Withdraw stands alone at the full width. A break does not stop withdrawals.
- The flag is AppShell's promoSuppressed, carried by the journey bar (onBreak) through WalletBalanceCaptioned to its
  sheet. It gates an OFFER and fails OPEN (feature-state.ts LAW 1): after a failed read the Deposit shows and the
  deposit screen still refuses. No sentence is added. The shell hands the browser one boolean and never the
  break's date, and the header drops its pill without one too.
- The classic capsule's Wallet is never told (classic chrome is frozen for S6/S7); its bar has no break rule either.

Guards: test:wallet-reach §7 "a reader on a BREAK gets no Deposit" (the column inside {!onBreak && (…)},
Withdraw outside it, the one-column rule) and 8c.1b "the break reaches the Wallet" (bar → capsule → journey sheet;
the classic sheet unchanged). 8.3's and 8c.1's pins follow the new line. red:wallet-reach has 3 new mutations: the
column leaves its guard, the bar stops passing the flag, the capsule drops it. One anchor moves with the line it
mutates.
