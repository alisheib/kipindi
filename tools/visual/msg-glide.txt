The Needle (R3-B follow-up): the host's rest glide advances whole engine steps per frame — no judder at top speed

Lock turn M7 ran test:needle-rest in a browser on the merged tip: 49/50. §1 "no teleport (change of step between
frames ≤ 6 px)" measured 6.50, on a 158px glide that otherwise passed (settles in 800ms, overshoot 0, max step 10.03).

- Cause, worked out on the vendored engine, not guessed. The engine moves the disc in fixed 8.33ms steps and carries
  leftover time between frames, and the host fed it each frame's real interval. When the leftover sits on a step
  boundary, frames a few tenths of a millisecond long or short alternate three steps then one: 3.3 → 10.0px at peak,
  a change of up to 6.72. Replayed over 2,520 jittered runs, the old host went over 6px in 355 of them (worst 6.72,
  worst step 10.03 — M7's figures). The spring's own motion at an even 60fps changes by at most 2.96. A dropped frame
  looks different (a ~33ms interval, velocity unchanged). It surfaced now because R3-B's clearance lengthened the
  glide: the artefact passes 6px from about 142px.
- needle-rest.ts (newGlideClock, glideFrame) and needle.tsx's tick, for the host's own rest glide only (a player's
  throw keeps real-time stepping). The first frame sets the time base with the leftover centred. After that the
  engine advances round(min(this interval, the previous one) / 8.33) whole steps: 2 per frame at 60Hz, 1 at 120Hz,
  whatever the jitter. A single dropped frame lengthens the glide by a frame instead of jumping.
- Also fixed: grabbing the disc mid-glide (pointer, bounce tap, Escape, Space/Enter, arrows) left the glide flag set,
  so the player's throw then landed with no haptic and no rest check. Each grab now calls endGlide().
- needle-rest §1's failure detail prints the two steps and their two frame intervals, so the next failure says which
  kind it is. The 6px limit is unchanged.

Replayed with the fix, 2,520 runs each, at 60Hz, 16.54ms, 16.80ms, 90, 120, 144 and 50Hz with ±0.3/1/2ms jitter: 158px
worst change 3.66, 0 over 6px; 246px (full reach) worst 5.71, 0 over 6px; durations ≤ 1.11s, overshoot 0, landing on
target; one dropped frame anywhere ≤ 3.14 (old host 10.79). Guard: test:needle-host §5 (68/68) — the old host's
judder as a plant, the fixed host across rates and jitter, a dropped frame, and pins for the tick's condition and the
grab handlers.
