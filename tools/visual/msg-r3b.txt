The Needle (R3-B): a resize mid-glide no longer strands the dial on the wrong edge; its rest clears text, controls and open panels by its glow; panels stack above it

Round 3 measured the dial wholly inside the viewport (296, 295), parked on the LEFT edge over row icons (304, 307,
315), mid-page on a button (299), touching a pill, a stat line and the 18+ ring (257, 265, 273, 194, 301, 309, 310),
and over the open channels panel (321). Measured again on the tiles, all confirmed.

- One cause for the first two: the rest glide sets an absolute target (needle.tsx), and on a resize applyViewport
  called reclamp(), which clamps a body that is not parked but leaves its target. The glide landed on the old width's
  tuck x, the engine judged it untucked, and it re-parked on the nearest edge by the disc's centre: after 390 → 1024
  the centre was at 392 < 512, so it parked LEFT. save() stored "left", and every later page restored it. Neither a
  drag nor a rest the logic chose.
- A new pure module, src/lib/needle-rest.ts (reseat): applyViewport reclamps only when the laid-out geometry changed.
  When the rail moves (width or diameter), any glide, park or peek finishes at its rest, tucked half off the SAME edge
  at the height it was heading for. A height-only change keeps the glide and clamps its end.
- Clearance (REST_TIERS), measured from the disc's box, which is the rim's ink within 1.2px. The glow reaches 10px at
  360, 11.4 at 390 and 25 on a 900px short side. Tier 0: nothing within glow + 4. Tier 1: content and text clear of
  glow + 4, with a card's frame allowed under it. Tier 2: the rim 8px off content and text. Tier 3: 4px (the old rule).
  Tier 4: 4px off every control, text accepted (E-413's floor). Text counts the painted box around it (the 18+ ring);
  small icons and images count too. A check that comes due mid-glide runs when the glide lands.
- Open surfaces: the channels panel, install card and consent card go to z-50, above the Needle (45) and below the
  menus (60). The rest counts open surfaces whole ([data-needle-keepout], [data-invitation], fixed or absolute
  dialogs, menus and listboxes), and a MutationObserver re-checks when one appears or goes.
- qa:journey-shell's shoot() waits up to 6 s for window.needle.resting() before each tile. A dial that never settles
  is a failing line, so a mid-glide tile like 194 is no longer captured as a rest.
