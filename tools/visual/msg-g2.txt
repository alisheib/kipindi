Visual pass G2: the Akaunti hub without bands or orphans, the profile invite's spacing, the footer pill, the channels panel's ×

The owner's rule (2026-10-08): only perfect visual and logical results. Every item was measured on the tiles with pixel
scans first.

- "Pendekeza na upate zawadi" wrapped beside its INAKUJA badge (tiles 124, 255, 297), because the badge took 84px of
  the label's line. Label and badge now share a head (.kp-hub__head). From 360 the badge drops under the label like a
  subtitle when both do not fit, and the row stays 56px. Below 360 it never wraps, which would make a 72px row. DOM
  order is unchanged.
- The guest "Msaada" subtitle wrapped word by word (73px row at 360 with "pepe" alone). HubSub splits a subtitle at the
  dictionary's own " · " into parts that never break inside themselves. The dots are decorative, centred in the gaps,
  and clipped at a line's start. The same applies to every dotted hub subtitle, which look the same on one line.
- The two-column hub opened 73px bands under shorter cards (257, 280, 258, 300, 125). It is now two flex columns, cut
  where they are most nearly level (hubColumnCut on wideRowCount's rows; the staff card always goes last on the
  right). DOM, keyboard and reading order are hubRowsFor's. Below 1024 the columns stack and phones are
  pixel-identical. The visible change: cards no longer pair side by side at 1024+.
- The footer's proposals link left its COMING SOON pill alone on a line at 1024 (299, 307) and at en 768. That link
  alone gets text-balance (FooterLink's new balance prop).
- /profile/invite: "MARAFIKI ZAKO" sat 6–7px under the buttons against the page's 28px rhythm (a !mt-1 override). The
  label, its note and the list are now one block taking the stack's 24px, with 12px between label and list. The diff
  is mostly re-indentation.
- Orphans: the zh card-size hint ("…任何内 / 容。") keeps its words whole (.kp-hub__sub:lang(zh)), and the guest lead
  ("zako.", "tickets.") is balanced.
- The channels panel's × sat 13.5px below the title's line and the first row 62.5px from the title against 46px
  between rows. It is now items-center with a 2px top gap: the × on the title's line and 46px everywhere. Class
  changes only.
