Money forms: the first line sits at the card's padding — the form's rhythm moves onto a wrapper React's injected input stays out of

Round 3 read the deposit form's first line still 51px below the card's border at 320/390 and 59px at 1280 (tiles
067–069). Tile 068 was pixel-identical to round 2, so G4's fix had not reached the browser. The cause G4 did not see:
React's server renderer writes <input type="hidden" name="$ACTION_ID_…"> straight after the <form> tag of every
server-action form (react-dom-server, pushAdditionalFormField, startHiddenInputChunk = '<input type="hidden"' — no
hidden attribute). Tailwind 3's space-y-5 on the form counted it as a sibling and put the 24px gap above the first
visible block. G4's hidden attribute on our own two inputs was right but not enough.

- deposit/page.tsx and withdraw/page.tsx: space-y-5 moves off the <form> onto one inner <div>, so the first child has no
  sibling before it whatever React adds to the form. The body is re-indented under the wrapper; git diff -w is +22/−3.
  The form keeps its classes (group/deposit, glass-panel, p-5 lg:p-6, withdraw's opacity-60).
- IdempotencyKeyField's note says what its hidden attribute still does (inside the wrapper) and what it could not do.
