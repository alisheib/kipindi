### 3.2 · The design (approved plan, refined by the code map)
**I3 becomes: at most one desk account per SIDE of a market, and one side per account** (OPPOSITE_SIDE unchanged).
Everything ships **OFF**: budget columns empty (= never), new per-account leaves default off.

**4b · PAIR (the core of "both sides").** When exactly ONE desk account holds ONE side of a LIVE Up & Down round, no
player money is on either side, and the opener's stake is PLACED, a SECOND account (different holder, `share.matchPct
> 0`, covering the chain) takes the OPPOSITE side at `floor(otherStake × matchPct / 100)`, never more than the other
account's open stake, clamped to its own stake rules, timed inside the price window, inside the budget.

**4a · otherSide (answer a player from a second account).** For an untargeted COUNTER whose account has
`share.otherSide = true`: OTHER_BOT refuses only when another account holds the SAME side as the counter (or has a live
row there); sizing stays against the PLAYER's money only (`lockedA15`). Openers, fills, Enter now and targets keep the
whole-market hold. A desk stake still never triggers another desk account's answer.
