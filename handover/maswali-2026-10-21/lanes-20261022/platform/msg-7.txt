MASWALI-BUILD S5-R2-PLATFORM 7: under either reduced-motion clamp an entrance's first frame is its end frame (the kit's entrances and the route's)

The motion critic's "Reduced-motion parity" row (7) had no owner until the lead's round 2. Its findings were a
hand-off flash and a line blank, which the slip and register lanes fixed on their own surfaces. Looking at the
platform level found the cause they share.

The universal clamp in motion.css zeroes an animation's time, not its first frame. A run it shortens to .01ms still
opens on its first keyframe, and Chromium paints that frame once before the run is over.
MEASURED (a scratch probe of motion.css in Chromium): each class was mounted with its element, and also restarted on
a standing element as RouteTransition does, under the OS preference and under the in-app switch. The first animation
frame showed:
  - `.m-seal` at opacity 0, scale .9;
  - `.m-axis-reveal` clipped away;
  - `.m-draw` undrawn (the dash still the path's length);
  - `.m-in`, `.m-in-lift`, `.m-dialog-in`, `.m-float-in` and `.m-scrim` at opacity 0;
  - `.m-sheet-in` a whole panel below its place;
  - `.route-enter` (the content wrapper, `m-settle-in`) at opacity 0.
That is 40 clamped first frames that hid or moved an entrance, on motion.css as it stood. The N-block and `.g-*`
started no run at all, because their branches are written. The control (no clamp) showed the first frames as designed.
So under either clamp, these stood invisible for a frame before their end frame:
  - a ticket's lock line, stamp line and perforation;
  - the unbroken line and the closing rule;
  - the slip's closed band;
  - every kit dialog, sheet and menu.
And every route change painted the new page's first frame blank: a blink between two pages, for the reader who asked
for no motion.
  - motion.css: their end frames, written for both clamps. Under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and under
    `html.kp-reduce-motion` each takes `animation: none`, so the layout's own frame is the first one painted. Each
    keyframe ends exactly on the layout.
      - `.m-draw` alone ends on a value its layout does not hold (its caller sets the dash inline), so its branch draws
        the line whole (`stroke-dashoffset: 0`, important over the inline style).
      - Exits are not branched. They open on the surface as it stands, and the kit unmounts a leaving surface at once
        under both clamps (exitBeatMs).
      - `.m-tick` and `.m-aura` are not branched either: each opens on the frame it ends on.
      - The `reduced` throttle tier is untouched; every entrance still plays there in full.
    The same probe on the new motion.css: 0 of 40.
  - test:reduce-motion rule 3. The set is DERIVED from the keyframes: every class rule of motion.css, every class a
    Millionea surface wears that names a one-shot animation, and the route entrance. A class is an ENTRANCE when its
    keyframe opens on another frame than its last, or names no first frame.
      - 3.0 premise: 76 keyframes read, 24 entrances derived, the dialog, the route entrance and Millionea's reveal and
        draw among them.
      - 3.1: both branches with `animation: none`.
      - 3.2: a keyframe with no first frame writes its last frame into both branches.
    red:reduce-motion gains three mutations: .m-seal out of the OS branch (3.1), the route entrance's in-app mirror
    pointed elsewhere (3.1), and the draw-on branch without its dash (3.2). 15/15 caught (was 12/12); src/ untouched.
  - qa:route-entrance 2.1 (the next server run): under both clamps, no frame at the new address paints the page below
    full opacity. 2′ plants the clamped run back and must see the blank frame.
  - DESIGN_AUTHORITY §M6 extends the law from mat/g/seal/crest to the kit's entrances, Millionea's and the route's,
    with the measurement.
