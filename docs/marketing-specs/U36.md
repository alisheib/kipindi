## Premises found false (8)

- "Its five doors" is wrong: there are SIX. U17 measured this (commit 7bef9f97). The page must carry its OWN gate as its whole returned body: admin-section-gate.test.mjs §0b/§0b′ (:67-105, PAGE_GATE_BODY at :97) refuses a page that relies on its layout. The layout must also be EXACTLY the literal wrapper: test:layout-staleness §1.0's SECTION_GATE_BODY (scripts/layout-staleness.test.mts:99-111) allows only a double-quoted literal prop, so the title cannot come from an imported constant.
- test:admin-nav cannot be THE guard. It has no red control: there is no --prove-red in scripts/admin-nav.test.mts and no red:admin-nav in package.json. It also cannot see the badge, the list, the progress or who gets the count. red:rbac's two cases are hard-wired to /admin/contacts (scripts/rbac.test.mts:271-291). §1's RED column for U36 therefore needs a new in-process suite (test:campaigns-page / red:campaigns-page), and red:rbac needs extending.
- The "SMS campaigns" label rule cannot be met through the nav label alone. The breadcrumb is title-cased from the URL segment (crumbsFromPath, admin-nav-groups.ts:67-75), and the restricted panel takes its heading from that crumb by default (src/components/admin/admin-section-gate.tsx:75). Both would print "Campaigns", the exact label §9 forbids, unless CRUMB_LABELS gains `campaigns: "SMS campaigns"` and both gates pass an explicit title="SMS campaigns" literal.
- "getSidebarBadges gains one count" would leak if done literally. The badges object is computed for EVERY staff viewer and serialised whole into the props of two client components (admin-shell.tsx:155, 168, 178, 242), whatever filterNavGroups hides. An unconditional campaign count would ship to the payloads of SUPPORT, FINANCE, MODERATOR, AUDITOR and COMPLIANCE. It must be gated the way canSeeMoney already is.
- The badge rule "running, or paused with outstanding rows" cannot be computed from U35's §9 text. U35 names a `status` without its values, and lists "timestamps" with no stamp for "enqueue finished". A PAUSED campaign whose enqueue walk never finished has zero outstanding ROWS but outstanding WORK, so it would silently drop off the badge. U36 needs U35 to name its status unions and to carry a nullable enqueue-finished stamp (`enqueuedAt`), or else to add one itself.
- The States line is incomplete. The filter-language door (§5.9) and §5.15 both require a status rail, and a rail filter that matches nothing is its own state: U21's trap, where an empty filter must keep its controls on screen. Add "no-match" to the line; it still names loading and error, so test:marketing-setup-plan §1c holds.
- U38's text says describeAudience appears "in the campaign row". U36 (S17) ships before U38 (S18), so the row can only show the confirmed `audienceCount` at U36. Adding the words to the row is U38's job and must be written into U38.
- §5.15's "counts from the same resolver the send uses (U24)" does not apply to this list. Row counts are a groupBy over SmsCampaignRecipient, and the audience figure is the `audienceCount` frozen at confirm (U40). The list never calls contactAudience, and inventing a second audience read here would be the two-paths defect OD36 forbids.
