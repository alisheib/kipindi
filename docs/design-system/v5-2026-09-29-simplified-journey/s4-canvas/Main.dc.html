Vodacom plan · S4 design pass · 2026-10-01

The agency's five screens, drawn in 50pick's own system

What happens on each screen — the flow, the order of things, every word — is the agency's, unchanged. How it looks is 50pick's: our navy ground and glass cards, our green and rose for YES and NO, gold only for money going in and for placing a bet, royal for paying, Sora for headlines, Inter for reading, JetBrains Mono for every figure. Below: each agency frame, and under it the same screen redrawn. This board maps each part to the 50pick component that draws it, and lists the three choices still open.

Reviewed on 1 October by four independent experts — our design system, the deck's logic, the words, and accessibility with responsible gambling. Their first-pass scores were 6.5, 6.5, 6 and 5 out of 10. Every finding was checked against the code and applied: the figures, the dates, the kit components, the words, eleven states that were missing, and the keyboard focus states.

The redrawn screens can be tapped through. Press Play on the 50pick Home frame, then tap NDIO on the Dodoma card, then 5,000, then "Weka pesa TZS 3,000", then back. The How-to card opens How to Play. The wallet logos are the ones 50pick already shows.

What never changes

The agency's colours, typeface and two-tone wordmark are not adopted (your ruling 5). Every word on these five screens is the deck's Swahili, verbatim. The figures are real: the Dodoma market at 13% gives ≈2.8× on the card, TZS 2,700 at TZS 1,000 and TZS 12,360 at TZS 5,000 — computed by the same code the server pays with. Every frame is dated from the deck's own day, Tuesday 29 September 2026, so "Inafungwa leo" and "Siku 11" agree everywhere.

The three choices — adopted on 1 October; switch any of them to overrule

CHOICE 1

How to Play step numbers

1A gold badges. 1B neutral badges, so gold appears only where money moves. Settled by our own rule: gold on a decorative element is a violation of the design authority (§M3), and the panel caught it, so 1B is now drawn.

Adopted: 1B. 1A stays on the switch only if you want to overrule the rule: How to Play frame, Tweaks › Choice 1.

CHOICE 2

The chosen stake amount

2A a royal ring, as drawn — our selection colour. 2B a filled light chip (white with dark text), which reads as "chosen" at a glance on a small phone.

Adopted: 2A — one selection colour across the product. Switch it on the Bet sheet or Deposit frame: Tweaks › Choice 2.

CHOICE 3

The play glyph on the How-to card

3A a royal plate, as drawn. 3B a neutral plate, quieter under the gold "Weka pesa" above it.

Adopted: 3A — the card is the first thing a new player should tap. Switch it on the Home frame: Tweaks › Choice 3.
