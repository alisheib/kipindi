How to play

  1. 1
    Choose a questionEvery question is a real event with one answer: YES or NO.
  2. 2
    Tap YES or NOSee right away what you could win, e.g. ≈2.7× your bet.
  3. 3
    Place your bet, wait for the resultWinners share the pool. Winnings go to your balance, and you withdraw them to mobile money.
Example

A TZS 1,000 bet on YES showing ≈2.7× → if you win you get about TZS 2,700. If you're wrong, you lose your bet.

Estimate. The final amount depends on the pool when the market closes. Our 13% commission on the losing side is already deducted.
