## 2. The rail: the final centre coin and Results moving into More
- **Slots:** Markets · Up & Down · **Deposit** · Live · More.
- **Results moves into More** in both branches. `moreActive` then marks More on `/results` with no extra code.
  - Player: Results, Positions, Wallet, Leaderboard, Invite, Propose.
  - Guest: Results, Leaderboard, Fairness, Propose.
- **Coin:** geometry per SPEC-VALUES §7 (final): 52px, rising 14px above the rail's top edge, with a 4px halo in `--panel` and no notch.
  - Ring: the mark's split, from −14°, green on the left and red on the right.
  - **Fill:** the same `.gilt-metal` struck ramp as the header pill, so money reads as one material; not flat gold.
  - A navy "+" and the mark's needle behind the fill.
  - Label `t.common.deposit`. The whole 56px slot is one link.
- **Route:** `/wallet/deposit` for everyone. Guests hit the existing auth gate.
- **Held wallet:** the slot becomes the Wallet door: `href="/wallet"`, label `t.nav.wallet`, and the coin shows the mark's hub instead of "+". This is the header's own rule: `/wallet/deposit` refuses a held wallet, so it gets no money-in control.
- **On `/wallet/deposit`:** the slot is current (`aria-current="page"`, label in `--text`).
- **Needle:** put `data-needle-keepout` on the coin element itself, because it rises out of the nav's rect.
- **Reserve:** keep the single `public-footer.tsx` reserve. Re-measure that the last content clears the coin (V22).
- **The rail's header comment:** list the new five slots and say Results lives in More. The centre is a destination (a route), so "destinations only" still holds. Record Ali's approval.
