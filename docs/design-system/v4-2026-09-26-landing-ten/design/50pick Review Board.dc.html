Panel ruling: where Deposit and Withdraw go

HEADER, SIGNED IN
A balance chip (TZS 12,400 ▾) next to a gold Deposit button. Tapping the chip opens the Wallet.
WALLET
Deposit and Withdraw side by side, the same size, with the amount that can be withdrawn now and a Set limits link. It opens as a bottom sheet on phones and a small panel on desktop.
ZERO BALANCE
No chip and no Withdraw button, since both would be empty. Deposit stays in the header, and the hero asks the player to add funds.
WHY
Players expect Deposit to be one tap away. The industry reviewer requires that withdrawing is never harder to find than depositing, so whenever the player has money, both sit together at equal size. The mobile reviewer puts both at thumb height.
