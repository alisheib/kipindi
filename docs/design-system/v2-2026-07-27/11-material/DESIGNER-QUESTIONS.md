# 50pick — questions for our graphic designer
From the material-system pass (Claude Design, 2026-08-06). Review the live spec
page first, then answer inline. Attach the full icon set and logo lockups with
your reply.

## What exists now (so you're judging the real thing)
- **One lamp**: light tilted −14° (the mark's axis), even 1px inner rings, washes
  at 166deg, shadows straight down. Five elevation rungs (flat → raised → float
  → modal → toast); every surface picks one.
- **Colors**: deep royal-blue field (oklch hue 268), pearl text, YES green /
  NO red, claret danger, and **champagne satin gold (chroma ≤0.10, hue 80–88)
  reserved exclusively for earned money** — payout, celebration, resolved seal.
  Nothing decorative wears gold. All color is oklch tokens; no hex anywhere.
- **Type**: Sora (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono for all money —
  tabular figures, never letter-spaced.
- **Motion law**: 6 durations / fixed easings, no loops outside a frozen ambient
  list, every animation has a reduced-motion end-frame. Icons only move on
  mount/data/state change — never hover. Identity (logo, crest, seal) MAY
  perform; glyphs may not.
- **The win moment**: modal lands (--t-move) → struck seal impresses at --t-max
  (milled gilt rim, guilloché enamel face, crown device over a −14° baton) →
  copy cascades 60ms apart → amount counts up on tabular figures and strikes to
  gilt at rest → bloom breathes ×2 → one specular sweep. No rays, no confetti.
- **The loss moment**: deliberately quiet — plain "factual" toast, no color, no
  tick. The settled card leads with the outcome ("Down wins"), dead timer
  demoted to a chip.

## Questions — mark-and-moment direction
1. **The crown device.** The seal face currently carries an arched crown over
   the −14° baton (my redraw). Keep the crown, replace with the real mark, or
   drop heraldry entirely for a pure coin face? What's the canon?
2. **Win identity gesture.** Options on the table: (a) the current struck seal,
   (b) a celebrating 50pick icon variant, (c) a coin-flip/spin of the mark that
   lands face-up. Which reads right for a regulated money product — and how
   theatrical may the win moment get before it feels casino?
3. **Loss identity.** Proposals heard: a countdown that starts at 100 and drains
   to 0, or a "sad" icon variant. Current stance is silence — no anthropomorphic
   sadness, no dwelling. Do you want ANY identity presence on a loss, and if so
   what tone: neutral receipt, brief acknowledgment, something else?
4. **The mark in motion.** The nav logo flips once (rotateY, single-shot, on
   mount and hover). Right gesture? Should the mark ever spin longer (e.g.
   while a round settles — a spinning coin as the "pending" state)?
5. **The −14° axis.** Is the tilt in the real mark exactly −14°? Everything
   (light angle, baton, dividers) is built on that number.
6. **Gold discipline.** Champagne satin (low chroma) replaced brass. Confirm
   the temperature — warmer/cooler? And do you agree gold stays earned-only,
   or does the brand want gold accents elsewhere?
7. **Icon set fit.** Our glyphs are 24px grid, stroke 1.9, rounded caps. Does
   your icon set match, or should the material pass restyle to yours?
8. **Overall direction.** In one line: is "royal mint / struck currency" the
   right metaphor for 50pick, or do you see a different material story?

Reply with numbers + any sketches. Motion specifics (durations, easing) stay
with us; we need your call on gesture, tone, and canon.
