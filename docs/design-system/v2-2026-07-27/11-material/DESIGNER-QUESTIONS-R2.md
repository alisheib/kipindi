## The questions — best approach, your call
1. **The seal's centre.** Right now the trademark itself is the celebration
   device. Alternative: a dedicated ceremonial variant (e.g. the disc with the
   needle standing fully vertical — "the market has resolved"), keeping the
   trademark pure for chrome. Which approach — trademark verbatim, a resolved
   pose, or something else?
2. **The needle as narrative.** The needle is the product's instrument
   (TippingBar, ConfidenceDial). Should the win moment use it — needle sweeps
   to the winning side before the flip? And on a loss, is a needle-settles-
   against-you gesture too pointed, or exactly honest?
3. **The flip's character.** One full rotation on the needle axis, ~700ms,
   settle easing. Feel: ceremonial (slow, weighty) or snappy (quick, coin-like)?
   May the hub/pivot catch a specular mid-flip, or does the mark stay flat art?
4. **Win amplitude ceiling.** Rank these from acceptable to too-much for a
   regulated money product: seal impress → mark flip → bloom → specular sweep →
   amount count-up. Where do we stop? Anything missing (e.g. a haptic-scale
   micro-shake on impress)?
5. **Loss identity.** Current stance: silence — no icon, no color, a factual
   receipt. The floated alternatives (100→0 drain, "sad" mark) read as dwelling
   to us. Confirm silence, or specify the one gesture a loss deserves.
6. **The two golds — which to keep where.** The system now carries TWO golds
   by design: the trademark's delivered **#E3BC66** (brand identity, hex,
   untouchable) and the surface system's **champagne satin** oklch ramp
   (88% · 0.085 · 88 → 72% · 0.100 · 80) for earned-money surfaces, ink and
   rims. They are close but not identical. Confirm the split — mark keeps its
   hex, everything else takes the satin ramp — or tell us to re-derive the
   satin ramp FROM #E3BC66 (oklch ≈ 79% · 0.095 · 84) so every gold in the
   product is a shading of the trademark's own metal. Our recommendation is
   the re-derivation: one gold, five shades — highlight 91%, body 84→76%,
   shade 68%, all at the mark's hue.
7. **The shading ladder, from the palette out.** Every surface today is a
   shading of royal-268: washes 28%→10% lightness, edges white at 5–9%
   alpha, shadows near-black at the same hue. Anything you'd re-balance —
   darker page field, brighter rung 4, more or less chroma in the depths?
8. **The mark on the enamel.** On the seal, the mark sits on deep royal
   guilloché inside a satin-gilt rim. Do the brand hexes hold up on that field,
   or does the seal placement want the white single-ink variant?
9. **Pending state.** BrandSpinner (PulseRing + mark) exists for loading.
   While a round settles — an anticipation moment, not a loading moment —
   may the mark spin continuously on its needle, or does the no-loops law hold
   even for identity?
10. **Icon set handoff.** Send the full set; confirm grid/stroke (ours: 24px,
    1.9, round caps) so the material pass can restyle or adopt.
