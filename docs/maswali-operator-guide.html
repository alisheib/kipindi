Maswali Millionea — Admin Guide

How Millionea works, how admins run it, and what every word on its console means. Labels in bold are the console's own. Rule references (MR-n, RULES §n) link to the rule that decides each point.

Part 1 · How Millionea works

A weekly ticket of fifteen YES/NO questions. Every ticket with all ten counted answers right shares the week's jackpot equally.

The weekA week sells from the moment an officer opens it to its close; its events run after the close. One public week sells at a time. The next week may open while the last is still being decided, and weeks settle strictly in calendar order. MR-3 · MR-4
The ticketA fixed price, paid from the main balance only — never bonus money. All fifteen questions are answered before paying, and the answers never change. Each account may hold up to the weekly cap. Staff accounts and accounts 50pick operates cannot buy. A ticket may be cancelled for a full refund inside the free-cancellation window, if that much selling time remained when it was bought; after the close nothing is bought or cancelled. MR-8 – MR-12 RULES §2.6
Ten counted of fifteenM1–M10 are the mains, A1–A5 the reserves. Ten are graded. A main that voids is replaced by the first reserve, in order, that has not voided and is not yet used; a voided reserve is skipped. If more mains void than there are usable reserves, the whole week voids and every ticket is refunded in full. A ticket wins only on 10 of 10 — except in a final week that no ticket wins (below). MR-17 · MR-18
The jackpotThe larger of the floor and the carry-in plus the week's sales. The floor is counted before the fee. In a winning week whose own money falls short of the floor, the guarantee — Owner capital — tops it up; the top-up is never carried forward. A week's carry-in joins it only once the week before it is final. MR-20 · MR-22
The fee at payoutA share of the jackpot, taken only when at least one ticket wins — nothing in a week without a winner. TRA's and GBT's levies come out of the fee, never out of a share. The rate is the one the week froze when it opened: the week page shows it under Rules this week froze, and Pools beside each open week (Fee at payout); the next week's is under Rules for the next week. MR-23 · MR-24 RULES §2.2
SharingThe jackpot less the fee, divided equally per winning ticket and rounded down to the shilling: two winning tickets, two shares. There is no minimum — a share can be smaller than the ticket price. The remainder, under one shilling per winner, joins the next jackpot as far as the week's own money covers it; the rest stays with the guarantee. MR-25
RolloverWith no 10 of 10, the week's whole sales join the jackpot carried: no fee, no cap — except in a final week (below). The carried jackpot is players' money, waiting for the next winning week. MR-21
The final weekMillionea ends only through a final week the Owner names. It runs normally. If no ticket gets 10 of 10, the tickets with the highest score share the jackpot: the fee applies, and there is no top-up. After a final week that ran, no public week opens again. MR-30
Who sees itThe Owner's rollout: Nobody (withdrawn), Staff preview or Everyone. A public week sells only to Everyone. A drive week is a private line for 50pick's own tests; it sells from Staff preview and never feeds a public jackpot. Withdrawing hides Millionea everywhere but a holder's own tickets, and never stops a settlement or a refund. MR-31
What is publishedAt the open, the question set's fingerprint. At the close, one root over every ticket still standing, with the count and the close time. Once the week is final, its register: every question, result, void and figure. A ticket's owner can check their ticket against the root. MR-13 MR-28

Every figure — the ticket price, the floor, the fee, the cap, the open and close, the buffer before the first event, the void deadline, the objection window — is a setting the Owner changes under Rules for the next week. A week freezes the whole set the moment it opens, and keeps it to the end. When asked about a week, read that week's own figures.

How every control behaves

  • A control your role cannot use is drawn locked, naming the officers who work it — "Finance officers only", "The Owner only" — and whom to ask.
  • Every act is checked again when it runs. A refusal is written where you act — inside its dialog when one is open — and nothing changes. Read it, fix the cause, try again.
  • If an act landed but its audit record could not be fully written, a warning says so: the act stands. Do not repeat it; tell an administrator.
  • Times are East Africa Time.
  • An act that changes money, results or the product asks you to type a word before it runs:

A · Before the first week — the Owner

A1 · Record the licence basis

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Licence basis. Choose the Basis — The Owner's dated ruling, The Gaming Board's letter or None (withdraw it) — write Where it is kept, press Record, type RECORD, press Record it.
Refused when
Where it is kept is missing or too long; the current record cannot be read.
If refused
Fill in where it is kept; try again. While none is recorded, no week can open.

A2 · Fund the guarantee MR-22

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Guarantee: Amount (TZS) and Bank or rail reference, then Pay in (type FUND GUARANTEE, Pay it in) or Take back (type RETURN GUARANTEE, Take it back).
Refused when
The reference was already used for a guarantee movement; a take-back would leave less than the unsettled weeks need; the balance cannot be read.
If refused
Use the bank's new reference; take back no more than the refusal names; try again later.

The guarantee must hold, for every unsettled week and the next one to open, the floor less that week's confirmed carry-in; no week opens until it does. The Pools tile Guarantee shows the balance and what is needed now. It is Owner capital, committed — never retained earnings.

A3 · Set the rules for the next week

Who
The Owner. Every other role reads them.
Where
Weeks → Rules for the next week: every figure the next week to open will freeze, under Money, The week, Results, The questions and The support desk. Change the fields, press Save the rules, read the list of changes, press Save the rules. Undo the changes puts the stored values back.
Refused when
A box is empty; a value breaks the rules' own bounds (the refusal names the field); the stored rules cannot be read.
If refused
Correct the named field and save again.

A week already open keeps the rules it froze. One field applies at once: Complaint answer target (working days), the support desk's promise.

A4 · Keep the public-holiday list

Who
The Owner. Every other role reads it.
Where
Weeks → Public holidays: Add a public holiday (EAT date) and Add; Remove beside a date; then Save the list and confirm.
Refused when
The list cannot be read, or a date is not a valid date.
If refused
Reload the page and save again.

A week whose sales close falls on a listed holiday shows a warning on its page while the close can still move. It is a warning, never a refusal: bring the close forward, or skip the week with notice.

A5 · Move the rollout

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Rollout: Visible to and Why, then Move the switch; type the state chosen; Move it. The environment's own ceiling still applies: the lower of the two is in force, and the Weeks tile Rollout shows it.
Refused when
The switch changed since the page loaded; it cannot be read; no reason is written.
If refused
Reload, read the state, and move it again.

B · A week's life

Finding a week

Weeks lists every week, newest first: search by Week code, filter by State and Line, press Apply (Clear resets). Sort by any heading: Week, Line, State, Sales close (EAT), Tickets, Settled. The list is paged; a week's code opens its page. Results says where its results stand (Part 3).

B1 · Draft a week MR-6 · MR-7

Who
Trading.
Where
Weeks → Draft a week. Choose The Monday the week starts (the next one by default) and the Line: Public or Drive (private). Start from the calendar lists fixtures and AI drafts in the week's window; Fill slot… copies one into a slot, and nothing is published. Fill all fifteen slots, M1–M10 and A1–A5 (see below), then press Save the draft.
Refused when
The week's sales close has already passed; a week with that code exists; a slot cannot be stored — the refusal names the slot (an event start in a clock change's skipped or repeated hour, a source that is not a web address).
If refused
Correct the named slot. For an existing week, open it from Weeks and edit it.

B2 · Edit or discard a draft

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Edit the questions → change the slots → Save the changes. Every edit clears the attestation. A draft that will never open is removed with Discard draft: type DISCARD, then Discard the draft. The draft and its fifteen questions are deleted and its Monday is free for a new draft; the discard is recorded.
Refused when
The week is no longer a draft; another officer saved a slot after you opened the editor (nothing was saved); nothing changed. A discard: the week has opened (a week that opened is never discarded), or it is the announced final week.
If refused
Reload the editor and make the change again. A refused discard stands: a week that opened, and the announced final week, are never discarded.

B3 · Attest the three languages

Who
A second Trading officer — never the one who drafted the week or edited it since its last attestation.
Where
The week page → Before it can open. Read all fifteen questions and criteria in English, Swahili and Chinese, press I read all three languages: they agree, type ATTEST, press Record the attestation. The card then reads The three languages are attested.
Refused when
You drafted or edited the draft; the questions changed after your page loaded; it is already attested; the draft's history cannot show who wrote it.
If refused
Reload and read all fifteen again, or ask another officer. A history that cannot be read goes to an administrator.

B4 · Open the week

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Open the week, which enables when Before it can open reads Every check passes: the week can open. Confirm Open the week for sale? The rules, the fifteen questions and their sources freeze; the question set's fingerprint is published; the week sells from that moment. Nothing opens a week by itself: open it at the time the rules set.
Refused when
Each reason is listed on the page: not ten mains and five reserves; not attested; the close has passed, or is less than the buffer before the first event; a category outside the market categories; politics in the English or Swahili text; a language missing its question or criterion; a void deadline after the week's final deadline; an estimated chance missing, without its source or outside the band; too many questions from one competition; reserves from too few sources; a source or fallback that is not trusted; two questions on one event; no licence basis; a guarantee short of what the unsettled weeks need; another week of the same line on sale; a later week of the line already opened; a final week standing; a final week before its announced open.
If refused
Fix each reason — an edit needs a new attestation. The Owner records the licence basis or pays into the guarantee. A week waits for the week on sale to close.

B5 · While it sells: move the close MR-4 · MR-5

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Move the sales close: New close date (EAT) and New close time (EAT), Move the close; type MOVE CLOSE; Move the close. Every holder is told the new close.
Refused when
Earlier, but inside the free-cancellation window plus a minute from now; later than the buffer before the first event (the refusal names the question); the close has passed; another officer moved it since your page loaded.
If refused
Choose a time inside the bounds, or reload and review the close.

An event brought forward: move the close to at or before its new start while that is still ahead. If tickets were sold after the event began, void that question once the week closes, on Settled or begun before the sales close. No ticket is ever graded on an event that began before it was bought.

B6 · While it sells: pause or resume sales

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Sales: Why sales pause, Pause sales, type PAUSE, Pause. To resume: Why sales resume, Resume sales, type RESUME, Resume.
Refused when
No reason is written; the pause record cannot be read.
If refused
Write the reason; try again.

A pause stops new tickets only; cancels, settlements and refunds go on. It is not a void and not a stop. Answers moving while a week sells names a question whose answers have swung among the latest tickets: check its event against its source before the close, and pause while you check.

B7 · The close

Who
The platform. Nobody presses anything.
Where
At the close the week becomes Closed: its root, ticket count and close time are stamped once and published, holders are told, and its page opens the seal console.
Refused when
Never. If the close has not been stamped a few minutes after it, Needs attention says so.
If refused
Tell engineering at once: nothing can be sealed until the close is stamped.

B8 · Seal the results MR-14 MR-19

Who
Trading — one officer per seal. An officer holding a ticket in the week cannot seal it.
Where
The week page → Seal the results. For each result that has landed, choose YES or NO under Result (Not yet until then), write what the source says under Evidence (ten characters at least), press the Seal button with its count, read the list, type SEAL, press Seal. Each row shows the question, how it resolves, its Source, its event time and void deadline — never ticket data. AI suggests (never seals) is only a suggestion.
Refused when
Evidence under ten characters; the question is already sealed; you hold a ticket in the week; the two-officer setting or the week's frozen rules cannot be read.
If refused
Write the evidence; correct an existing seal instead (B11); ask another officer; try again.

Seal all fifteen, reserves in order: a later void can pull an unused reserve in, and it must already carry its result. When the counted set is final, the objection window opens. Alerts fire 2 and 12 hours after an event started with its question still unsealed.

B9 · Void a question MR-15

Who
Trading.
Where
The question's Void…: choose the Ground, write the Evidence, type VOID, press Void the question. A voided main is replaced by the next usable reserve; a voided reserve is skipped. Holders are told.
Refused when
The ground needs the void deadline and it has not passed; the question is already sealed; you hold a ticket in the week; evidence under ten characters.
If refused
Wait for the void deadline (the row shows it, and void proposed once it passes); choose the ground that fits; correct an existing seal instead.
GroundWhen it applies
Not played by its void deadlineNot played or not finished by the deadline printed on the question. Only once the deadline has passed.
Abandoned, no official resultAbandoned, and the source publishes no result.
No reading from the sourcesNeither the source nor the fallback gave a reading by the deadline. Only once the deadline has passed.
Settled or begun before the sales closeThe outcome was public, or the event had begun, before sales closed.
Criterion wrong, or the texts divergeThe criterion is materially wrong, or the English and Swahili say different things; found before the close.
Criterion wrong, found after the closeThe same, found after the close and before the objection window ends.

There is no other ground: no "management may refund", and a fault on 50pick's side never voids one question.

B10 · Confirm a seal as second officer

Who
A second Trading officer — never the one who sealed it, never one holding a ticket in the week.
Where
A seal made while the platform's two-officer setting was on reads awaits a second officer and does not count until confirmed: its Confirm as second officer, type CONFIRM, Confirm this seal.
Refused when
The seal changed since your page loaded; you sealed it; it is already confirmed; you hold a ticket in the week; the week's results are final.
If refused
Reload and read the result again, or ask another officer.

B11 · Correct your own seal MR-16

Who
Trading — the officer who sealed the question.
Where
While the objection window is open, the question's Correct…: the Correct result (YES, NO, or VOID with its ground) and the evidence; type CORRECT; Record the correction. The window starts again once the results are final; a seal that needed a second officer needs one again.
Refused when
The objection window has closed — the seal stands; you did not seal it; the seal changed since your page loaded; evidence under ten characters; a void ground not yet available.
If refused
After the window, the result stands. Anyone else raises a doubt as in B12.

B12 · Rule on an objection MR-16

Who
Compliance — never the officer who sealed the question or confirmed its seal, nor the one whose upheld ruling set its present result, nor the objector, nor an officer holding a ticket in the week. The Owner rules when no other officer can.
Where
A holder objects to one question from their ticket's page while the objection window is open. Integrity → Objections lists each week's objections by question: the result under dispute, each filer's reason and words. Choose Reject…, Reverse…, Void… (with its ground) or Reinstate… (with its result), write the Reason for the record — the player reads it — and type the ruling's word. An open objection holds its week's settlement and its week void. An upheld ruling that changes the counted set grades the week again and restarts the objection window, once per week.
Refused when
You are barred on that question — the card says why in place of the buttons; the result changed since your page loaded; no reason for the record.
If refused
Another Compliance officer rules, or the Owner when none can. Reload and read the result again.

B13 · Release the settlement MR-19 MR-26

Who
Finance.
Where
Pools → Open weeks. Once a week's results are final and its objection window has closed, its Settlement shows the prepared figures: the winners, each share, the fee and the top-up — or what rolls to the jackpot. Press Release (the button names the week), type RELEASE, press Release the settlement. It pays exactly those figures, in one transaction.
Refused when
The figures changed since they were prepared; the guarantee holds less than the top-up; an earlier week of the line is not settled; an objection stands; the week's sales do not agree three ways (nothing is paid).
If refused
Reload Pools and read the new figures. The Owner pays into the guarantee, then release again. Settle the earlier week first. A disagreement in sales goes to engineering.

A winner the settlement cannot pay freely — a staff account, a suspended or closed account, a deposit under review — is paid and held for Compliance; the row says how many (B15). Before releasing, compare Payout float with its prepared payouts; Finance tops the float up when short. An alert fires when a week waits too long for its release. Find an open week with Find an open week by code and Find week, the All lines · Public · Drive and All states · Selling · Closed · Sealing filters, or sort by Week, State, Sales, Coverage or Worst top-up (newest week first). Each row shows the fee its week froze, under Fee at payout.

B14 · Screen the winners

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Winners: every paid ticket with its account. Search by Week or ticket serial, filter Screening (All winners, Flagged, Not flagged), sort by Week, Ticket or Share. On a row, Hold…, write why, type HOLD, Hold it: the winner's wallet is held until the share is decided. Possible account takeovers after a payout names a winning account that may have changed hands since its payout, for Compliance to review; its share can be held from its row here. Nothing else stops the money.
Refused when
The share is already held or forfeited; the ticket is not a paid winning ticket; no reason.
If refused
Decide it under Held shares (B15).

B15 · Decide a held share

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Held shares, waiting decisions first. Search by Week, ticket or holder, filter Decision (All decisions, Pending, Forfeited), sort by any heading. Release… (type RELEASE, Release it): the player keeps the share, and the wallet's hold lifts once no other share of theirs is held. Forfeit… (type FORFEIT SHARE, Forfeit to the jackpot): the whole share goes back to the jackpot for the next winning week — never to 50pick. Both need The decision in words.
Refused when
The share is not held; the wallet no longer holds the share (forfeit); no decision written.
If refused
Read the account's wallet with Finance before deciding again.

B16 · Pay a winner who cannot sign in

Who
Compliance, when the holder asks to be paid.
Where
Integrity → Winners who cannot sign in: each share owed to a winner whose account is self-excluded, closed or suspended, with what is paid and what is left, until it is paid in full. When the holder asks, press Pay…, choose The holder's mobile-money network, write How and when the holder asked to be paid, type PAY SHARE and press Pay to the registered number. The money goes only to the number registered on the account, at the usual withdrawal fee and within the most one withdrawal may send: a larger share is paid in equal parts, one press each. The account's own hold stays, and nothing else leaves the wallet.
Refused when
The holder has never verified their identity or has no confirmed email address (the withdrawal rules do not change for this); the share is held or forfeited; the wallet carries another hold; a payment of the share is still in flight; the payout rail is not taking requests; the account can sign in (its holder withdraws the share); the share is your own.
If refused
The row says why before you press. Ask the holder to verify their identity or confirm an email address, decide the other hold on its own control, or wait for the payment in flight, then pay again.

B17 · Reach every winner

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Winners not yet reached: recent winners whom no notice is known to have reached, with what was sent. Call them, then Reached by phone…, say how they were reached, type REACHED, Record the call. A winner leaves the list once they open the result in the app, their SMS is delivered, or a call is recorded. An alert fires when a winner is still unreached a day after the settlement.
Refused when
The week is not settled; the account holds no winning ticket of that week; the note is too short; the record could not be written (the winner stays listed).
If refused
Try again.

B18 · Letters not known to have gone

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Letters not known to have gone: a week's win or refund letters that the email provider may have taken without saying so. Check the provider's activity, then Checked…, say what was checked, type CHECKED, Record the check: those letters are closed for good and never sent again.

B19 · Void a whole week MR-18

Who
Compliance — or the platform by itself.
Where
Integrity → Weeks that void whole: a week whose sealed record makes the void certain and whose objection window has closed. Void (the button names the week), type VOID, Void and refund: every live ticket is refunded in full, no fee is taken, and the jackpot carried is untouched. A doomed week stops selling at once, but its void waits for its objection window.

B20 · Download a week's evidence

Who
Compliance, the Auditor, the Owner.
Where
Evidence: every week that has opened, newest first. Find a week by its code, filter State (All states, or one state) and Line (Both lines, Public, Drive), Apply; sort by Week, State, Sales closed (EAT) or Tickets. B21 · Look up a ticket for a player

B23 · Stop Millionea MR-30

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Stop the product: The final week (Find the final week by code when there are many) and Why Millionea is stopping, then Make it the final week, type SUNSET MILLIONEA, Make it final. Three ways: a draft named a full week before it opens; the week on sale, at once; while a week's results are being decided, the next week to open, at shorter notice. With no week to offer, the card names the next act.
Refused when
A drive week; a week whose sales have closed (it settles under its own rules); a draft opening in less than a full week, unless it is the next week while another is being decided; a later week already opened; a final week already standing.
If refused
Follow the next act the refusal names.

A final week never opens before its announced time. Pausing sales, voiding a week and stopping Millionea are three different acts.

Weeks and questions

Week codeA public week reads MLN-year-Wweek; a drive week MLD-….
Public · DriveThe week's line: on sale to players, or private for 50pick's own tests.
DraftBeing written; nothing is on sale.
SellingOpen: tickets can be bought.
ClosedSales have closed; no result is sealed yet.
SealingFrom the first seal until the week settles or voids: the seals, the objection window, the release.
SettledPaid out, or rolled over. Final.
VoidedVoided whole; every ticket refunded in full.
Final (beside a code)The final week: Millionea ends after it.
Results: N of 15 sealed · needs NSealed so far, and how many more the settlement waits for.
Results: Final · window to …The counted set is final; the objection window ends then.
Voids wholeMore mains voided than usable reserves: the week voids and refunds.
Main · reserve (Akiba)M1–M10 are graded; A1–A5 stand in, in order, for mains that void.
Counted setThe ten questions a ticket is graded on.
SealAn officer's recorded result for one question — YES, NO or VOID — with its evidence.
Not yetNo result chosen for that question.
void proposedIts void deadline passed unsealed; an officer seals the void.
awaits a second officerA two-officer seal not yet confirmed; it does not count yet.
Void deadlineThe latest time a question's event may be played or read; printed on the question.
Final deadlineThe latest any void deadline may fall, in the week after the close.
Objection windowOpens when the counted set is final; holders may object while it is open, and nothing is paid. A correction, or an upheld ruling that changes the counted set, restarts it.
Objection: Open · settlement frozen · Upheld · RejectedWaiting for a ruling, and holding the week's settlement; ruled for the holder; ruled against.
AttestationA second reader's record that the three languages agree. An edit clears it.
Fingerprint · root · commitmentThe question set's fingerprint at the open; the root over every ticket at the close, with the count and time — the commitment no later change can hide.
RegisterThe public record of each final week: questions, results, voids, money.

Money

JackpotWhat a winning week shares: the floor, or the carry-in plus the week's sales, whichever is larger.
FloorThe smallest jackpot a winning week pays, counted before the fee.
Fee at payoutThe week's share of the jackpot, taken only when a ticket wins; TRA and GBT come out of it.
ShareOne winning ticket's part of the jackpot after the fee; equal for every winning ticket.
Rounding carriedThe few shillings an equal split leaves over; shown on the register.
Jackpot carriedPlayers' money from weeks nobody won, waiting for the next winning week.
GuaranteeOwner capital backing the floor; beside it, what the unsettled weeks need now.
Top-upWhat the guarantee adds to a winning week to reach the floor.
Payout floatThe money winners withdraw from; beside it, the payouts already prepared.
Drive jackpotThe private drive line's own jackpot.
SalesLive tickets × the week's ticket price.
Carry inThe jackpot a week claimed when the week before it became final; Not yet until then.
CoverageHow many of the 1,024 ways to answer the ten mains some live ticket holds.
Worst top-upThe most the week could still draw from the guarantee.
SettlementThe prepared figures and the release; Waiting on the objection window. or Not ready. before then.
Winners · Fee · Top-up · PaidA settled week's winning tickets, fee, guarantee top-up and total paid.
Carried onWhat the settled week left in the jackpot for the next.
Levy on own top-upThe TRA and GBT levies on the fee the guarantee's own top-up created, paid from 50pick's money.
Cannot be readThe figure is unknown — never zero.
