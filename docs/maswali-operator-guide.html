Maswali Millionea — Admin Guide

How Millionea works, how admins run it, and what every word on its console means. Words in bold are the console's own.

Part 1 · How Millionea works

A weekly ticket of fifteen YES/NO questions. Every ticket with all ten counted answers right shares the week's jackpot equally. This part sums up the binding rules players accept, published at 50pick.tz/legal/rules/millionea.

The weekA week sells from the moment an officer opens it until its sales close; its events run after the close. One public week sells at a time. The next week may open while the last is still being decided, and weeks settle strictly in calendar order.
The ticketA fixed price, paid from the main balance only — never bonus money. All fifteen questions are answered before paying, and the answers never change. Each account may hold up to the weekly cap. Staff accounts and accounts 50pick operates cannot buy, and an account holding a ticket cannot be made staff until its week settles. A ticket may be cancelled for a full refund inside the free-cancel window, if that much selling time remained when it was bought; after the close nothing is bought or cancelled.
Ten counted of fifteenM1–M10 are the mains, A1–A5 the reserves. Ten are graded. A main that voids is replaced by the first reserve, in order, that has not voided and is not yet used; a voided reserve is skipped. If more mains void than there are usable reserves, the whole week voids and every ticket is refunded in full. A ticket wins only on 10 of 10 — except in a final week that no ticket wins (below).
The jackpotThe larger of the floor and the carry-in plus the week's sales. The floor is counted before the fee. In a winning week whose own money falls short of the floor, the guarantee — Owner capital — tops it up; the top-up is never carried forward. A week's carry-in joins it only once the week before it is final.
The fee at payoutA percentage of the jackpot, taken only when at least one ticket wins — nothing in a week without a winner. TRA's and GBT's levies come out of the fee, never out of a share. The rate is the one the week froze when it opened: the week page shows it under Rules this week froze, and Pools beside each open week (Fee at payout); the next week's is under Rules for the next week.
SharingThe jackpot less the fee, divided equally per winning ticket and rounded down to the shilling: a player with two winning tickets gets two shares. There is no minimum — a share can be smaller than the ticket price. The remainder, under one shilling per winning ticket, joins the next jackpot as far as the week's own money covers it; the rest stays with the guarantee.
RolloverWith no 10 of 10, the week's whole sales join the jackpot carried: no fee, no upper limit — except in a final week (below). The carried jackpot is players' money, waiting for the next winning week.
The final weekMillionea ends only through a final week the Owner names. It runs normally. If no ticket gets 10 of 10, the tickets with the highest score share the jackpot: the fee applies, and there is no top-up. If the final week voids or sells nothing, the next public week to open becomes the final week by itself. Once a final week has sold and settled, no public week opens again.
Who sees itThe Owner's rollout: Nobody (withdrawn), Staff preview or Everyone, under a ceiling the technical team sets on the server; the lower of the two is in force. A public week sells only at Everyone. At Staff preview only staff see Millionea, and staff cannot buy. Withdrawing stops every sale and hides Millionea everywhere but a holder's own tickets; it never stops a cancel in the free-cancel window, an objection, a settlement or a refund.
What is publishedAt the open, the question set's fingerprint. At the close, one root over every ticket still standing, with the count and the close time. Once the week settles or voids, its register: every question, result, void and figure. A ticket's owner can check their ticket against the root.

Every figure — the ticket price, the floor, the fee, the cap, the sales open and close, the buffer before the first event, the void deadline, the objection window — is a setting the Owner changes under Rules for the next week. A week takes its sales open and close from these rules when it is drafted; it freezes the rest of these figures the moment it opens, and keeps them to the end. When asked about a week, read its own figures: Sales open and Sales close at the top of its page, the rest under Rules this week froze.

How every control behaves

  • A control your role cannot use is drawn locked, naming who works it — "Finance officers only", "The Owner only"; its tooltip says whom to ask.
  • Every act is checked again when it runs. A refusal is written where you act, and nothing changes (a batch of seals keeps the rows before the refused one: B8): read it, fix the cause, try again.
  • If an act landed but its audit record could not be fully written, a warning says so: the act stands. Do not repeat it; tell an administrator.
  • "An administrator" is 50pick's technical team. Times are East Africa Time (EAT).

A · Before the first week — the Owner

A1 · Record the licence basis

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Licence basis. Choose the Basis — The Owner's dated ruling, The Gaming Board's letter or None (withdraw it) — write Where it is kept (not asked for None), press Record, type RECORD, press Record it.
Refused when
Where it is kept is too short or too long (the button stays off until it fits); the current record cannot be read; the record could not be stored.
If refused
Correct the text; reload and try again. While none is recorded, no week can open.

A2 · Fund the guarantee

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Guarantee: Amount (TZS) and Bank or rail reference, then Pay in (type FUND GUARANTEE, Pay it in) or Take back (type RETURN GUARANTEE, Take it back).
Refused when
The reference was already used for another pay-in (or take-back); a take-back would leave less than the unsettled weeks need; the balance, the guarantee's records or a week's frozen rules cannot be read.
If refused
Use the bank's new reference; take back no more than the refusal names; try again later.

The guarantee must hold, for every unsettled week and the next one to open, the floor less that week's confirmed carry-in; no week opens until it does. The Pools tile Guarantee shows the balance and what the weeks already open need; a draft's own page names what its open needs. It is the Owner's capital, committed to Millionea.

A3 · Set the rules for the next week

Who
The Owner. Trading and the Auditor read them there.
Where
Weeks → Rules for the next week: every figure the next week to open will freeze, under Money, The week, Results, The questions and The support desk. Change the fields, press Save the rules, read the list of changes, press Save the rules. Undo the changes puts the stored values back.
Refused when
A box is empty; a value breaks the rules' own bounds (the refusal says which rule); the stored rules cannot be read.
If refused
Correct the value and save again.

A week already open keeps the rules it froze, and a draft keeps the sales open and close it was drafted with. Two fields apply at once to every week: Complaint answer target (working days), the support desk's promise, and Alert when a release waits (hours).

A4 · Keep the public-holiday list

Who
The Owner. Trading and the Auditor read it there.
Where
Weeks → Public holidays: Add a public holiday (EAT date) and Add; Remove beside a date; then Save the list and confirm.
Refused when
The list cannot be read, or a date is not a valid date.
If refused
Reload the page and save again.

A week whose sales close falls on a listed holiday shows a warning on its page while the close can still move. It is a warning, never a refusal: bring the close forward, or skip the week with notice.

A5 · Move the rollout

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Rollout: Visible to and Why, then Move the switch; type the state as the dialog names it — WITHDRAWN, STAFF_PREVIEW or ACTIVE; Move it. The Weeks tile Rollout shows the state in force.
Refused when
The switch changed since the page loaded; it cannot be read; the reason is too short or too long; the change could not be stored.
If refused
Reload, read the state, and move it again.

B · A week's life

Finding a week

Weeks lists every week, newest first: search by Week code, filter by State and Line, press Apply (Clear resets). Sort by Week, Line, State, Sales close (EAT), Tickets or Settled. The list is paged; a week's code opens its page, and Final marks the final week. Results says where its results stand (Part 3).

B1 · Draft a week

Who
Trading.
Where
Weeks → Draft a week. Choose The Monday the week starts (the next one by default) and the Line: Public — the drive line is not in use. Start from the calendar lists fixtures and AI drafts in the week's window; Fill slot… copies one into a slot, and nothing is published. Fill all fifteen slots, M1–M10 and A1–A5 (see below), then press Save the draft.
Refused when
The date is not a Monday; the week's sales close has already passed; a week for that Monday and line exists; a slot cannot be stored — the refusal names the slot (no event date or time, a start in a clock change's skipped or repeated hour, a source that is not a web address).
If refused
Correct the named slot. For an existing week, open it from Weeks and edit it.
Field, for each slotWhat to enter
Category · Kind of eventThe market category, and the kind of event, which picks the criterion's template. A question about politics never opens.
Question · English, Question · Kiswahili, Question · 中文One plain YES/NO question per language. The counter is a guide for the player's screen, not a limit.
How it resolves · English (and Kiswahili, 中文)The criterion, exactly as the source will settle it. The template button fills the English; fill every {…} — nothing refuses one left empty.

Estimated chance of YES (%) · Where the estimate comes fromA chance inside the rules' band, and its source.

Keep to one question per event, and to the rules' limit of questions from one competition: the open refuses only the same question twice.

B2 · Edit or discard a draft

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Edit the questions → change the slots → Save the changes. Every edit clears the attestation. The Monday and the line cannot be edited: to change them, discard the draft and draft it again. Discard draft, type DISCARD, Discard the draft: the draft and its fifteen questions are deleted, its Monday is free for a new draft, and the discard is recorded. The announced final week is never discarded.
Refused when
The week is no longer a draft (a week that opened is never discarded); another officer saved a slot after you opened the editor (nothing was saved); nothing changed; a slot cannot be stored, as in B1.
If refused
Reload the editor and make the change again.

B3 · Attest the three languages

Who
A second Trading officer — never the one who drafted the week or edited it since its last attestation.
Where
Open Edit the questions and read all fifteen questions and their criteria in English, Kiswahili and Chinese; save nothing. Back on the week page, under Before it can open, press I read all three languages: they agree, type ATTEST, press Record the attestation. The card then reads The three languages are attested.
Refused when
You drafted or edited the draft; the questions changed after your page loaded; it is already attested; the draft's history cannot be read or does not show who wrote it.
If refused
Reload and read all fifteen again, or ask another officer. If the refusal says the history needs an administrator, tell one.

B4 · Open the week

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Open the week, which enables when Before it can open reads Every check passes: the week can open. Confirm Open the week for sale? The rules, the fifteen questions and their sources freeze; the question set's fingerprint is published; the week sells from that moment, while the rollout is Everyone and sales are not paused. Nothing opens a week by itself: open it at its Sales open time.
Refused when
Before it can open lists every reason: not attested; the close has passed, or falls inside the buffer before the first event; a question about politics; a language missing its question or criterion; an estimated chance missing, without its source or outside the band; reserves from too few sources; a source or fallback that is not trusted; the same question twice; the rules do not validate; no licence basis; a guarantee short of what the unsettled weeks need. The press itself is refused when another week of the same line is on sale; a later week of the line has already opened; Millionea has ended, or the week comes after the final week; the final week is pressed before its announced open.
If refused
Fix each reason — an edit needs a new attestation. The Owner corrects the rules, records the licence basis or pays into the guarantee. A week waits for the week on sale to close. A draft whose close has passed, or that a later week has overtaken, never opens: discard it.

B5 · While it sells: move the close

Who
Trading.
Where
The week page → Move the sales close: New close date (EAT) and New close time (EAT), Move the close; type MOVE CLOSE; Move the close. Every holder is told the new close.
Refused when
Earlier than the free-cancel window plus one minute from now; later than the buffer before the first event (the refusal names the question); the close has passed; another officer moved it since your page loaded.
If refused
Choose a time inside the bounds, or reload and review the close.

An event brought forward: move the close to at or before its new start while that is still ahead. If tickets were sold after the event began, void that question once the week closes, on Settled or begun before the sales close. No ticket is ever graded on an event that began before it was bought.

B6 · While it sells: pause or resume sales

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Sales: Why sales pause, Pause sales, type PAUSE, Pause. To resume: Why sales resume, Resume sales, type RESUME, Resume.
Refused when
The reason is too short or too long; the pause record cannot be read.
If refused
Write the reason; try again.

A pause stops every new ticket; cancels, settlements and refunds go on, and the close does not move. It is not a void and not a stop. Answers moving while a week sells names a question to check: check its event against its source before the close, and pause sales while you check. If the event moved, Trading moves the close (B5).

B7 · The close

Who
The platform, at the close time. Nobody presses anything.
Where
Sales stop at the close time. The week then becomes Closed: its root, ticket count and close time are stamped once and published, holders are told, and its page opens the seal console.
Refused when
Never. If the close is not stamped five minutes after the close time, Needs attention says so.
If refused
Tell an administrator at once: sales have stopped, but nothing can be sealed until the close is stamped.

B8 · Seal the results

Who
Trading — one officer per seal.
Where
The week page → Seal the results. For each result that has landed, choose YES or NO under Result (Not yet until then), write what the source says under Evidence (at least 10 characters), press the Seal button with its count, read the list, type SEAL, press Seal. Each row shows the question, how it resolves, its Source, its event time and void deadline — never ticket data.
Refused when
Evidence under 10 characters; the question is already sealed; the two-officer setting or the week's frozen rules cannot be read. A batch stops at a refused row: the rows before it stay sealed, and the page names them.
If refused
Write the evidence; correct an existing seal instead (B11); fix the refused row and seal the rest.

Seal all fifteen, reserves in order: a later void can pull an unused reserve in, and it must already carry its result. The Results tile names the slots still needed. When the graded set is final, the objection window opens. Alerts fire 2 and 12 hours after an event started with its question still unsealed.

B9 · Void a question

Who
Trading.
Where
The question's Void…: choose the Ground, write the Evidence, type VOID, press Void the question. A voided main is replaced by the next usable reserve; a voided reserve is skipped. Holders are told.
Refused when
The ground needs the void deadline and it has not passed; the question is already sealed; evidence under 10 characters.
If refused
Wait for the void deadline (the row shows it, and void proposed once it passes); choose the ground that fits; correct an existing seal instead.
GroundWhen it applies
Not played by its void deadlineNot played or not finished by the deadline printed on the question. Only once the deadline has passed.
Abandoned, no official resultAbandoned, and the source publishes no result.
No reading from the sourcesNeither the source nor the fallback gave a reading by the deadline. Only once the deadline has passed.
Settled or begun before the sales closeThe outcome was public, or the event had begun, before sales closed.
Criterion wrong, or the texts divergeThe criterion is materially wrong, or the English and Kiswahili say different things; found before the close.
Criterion wrong, found after the closeThe same, found after the close and before the objection window ends.

There is no other ground: a fault on 50pick's side never voids one question.

B10 · Confirm a seal as second officer

Who
A second Trading officer — never the one who sealed it.
Where
A seal made while the platform's two-officer setting was on (Compliance's "Two-admin authorization" switch, on the Resolver queue) reads awaits a second officer and does not count until confirmed: its Confirm as second officer, type CONFIRM, Confirm this seal.
Refused when
The seal changed since your page loaded; you sealed it; it is already confirmed; the week has settled or voided.
If refused
Reload and read the result again, or ask another officer.

B11 · Correct your own seal

Who
Trading — the officer who sealed the question.
Where
While the objection window is open, the question's Correct…: the Correct result (YES, NO, or VOID with its ground) and the evidence; type CORRECT; Record the correction. The window starts again once the graded set is final again; a seal that needed a second officer needs one again.
Refused when
The objection window has closed — the seal stands; you did not seal it; the seal changed since your page loaded; evidence under 10 characters; a void ground not yet available.
If refused
After the window, the result stands. Only the officer who sealed can correct a seal: another officer who doubts one tells its sealer while the window is open; a holder objects (B12).

B12 · Rule on an objection

Who
Compliance — never the officer who sealed the question or confirmed its seal, nor the one whose upheld ruling set its present result. The Owner rules when no other officer can.
Where
A holder objects to one question from their ticket's page while the objection window is open. Integrity → Objections lists each week's objections by question: the result under dispute, each filer's reason and words. On a YES or NO result choose Reject…, Reverse… or Void… (with its ground); on a void one, Reject… or Reinstate… (with its result). Write the Reason for the record — the player reads it — and type the ruling's word. An open objection holds its week's settlement and its week void. An upheld ruling that changes the graded set grades the week again; the first such ruling also restarts the objection window, once the graded set is final again. An upheld void may first need a reserve sealed, or may void the whole week.
Refused when
You are barred on that question — the card says why in place of the buttons; another officer ruled first, or the result changed since your page loaded; the void ground is not yet available; the reason for the record is under 10 characters.
If refused
Another Compliance officer rules, or the Owner when none can. Reload and read the result again.

B13 · Release the settlement

Who
Finance.
Where
Pools → Open weeks. Once a week's graded set is final and its objection window has closed, its Settlement shows the prepared figures: the winners, each share, the fee and the top-up — or what rolls to the jackpot. Press Release (the button names the week), type RELEASE, press Release the settlement. It pays exactly those figures, in one transaction.
Refused when
The figures changed since they were prepared; the guarantee holds less than the top-up; an earlier week of the line is not settled; an objection stands; the jackpot holds less than the carry the week claimed; the week's sales figures do not match. Nothing is paid.
If refused
Reload Pools and read the new figures. The Owner pays into the guarantee, then release again. Settle the earlier week first. A short jackpot or a disagreement in sales goes to an administrator.

A winner the settlement cannot pay freely — a staff account, a suspended or closed account, a deposit under review, an account that could not be checked — is paid and held for Compliance; the row says how many (B15). Beside each release the row sets the payout float against that week's shares and the headroom kept for withdrawals: when it is short or cannot be read, top the float up or have it checked first. The release never refuses for the float — it credits every winner in full, and some may wait to withdraw. A week left unreleased past the release-alert hours raises an alert (C). Find an open week with Find an open week by code and Find week, the All lines · Public · Drive and All states · Selling · Closed · Sealing filters, or sort by Week, State, Sales, Coverage or Worst top-up (newest week first).

B14 · Screen the winners

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Winners: every paid ticket with its account. Search by Week or ticket serial, filter Screening (All winners, Flagged — held or forfeited — or Not flagged), sort by Week, Ticket or Share. On a row, Hold…, write why, type HOLD, Hold it: the winner's wallet is frozen — no money moves in or out — until the share is decided. Two cards only alert, and the share is held from its row here: Possible account takeovers after a payout, a winning account whose owner must be confirmed; and, before a week pays, a group under Connected accounts that holds a winning ticket — review it before the release, and hold any share you doubt once the week pays.
Refused when
The share is already held or forfeited; the ticket is not a paid winning ticket; the reason is too short or too long.
If refused
Decide it under Held shares (B15).

B15 · Decide a held share

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Held shares, waiting decisions first. Search by Week, ticket or holder, filter Decision (All decisions, Pending, Forfeited), sort by any heading but Why. Release… (type RELEASE, Release it): the player keeps the share, and the wallet's hold lifts once no other share of theirs is held. Forfeit… (type FORFEIT SHARE, Forfeit to the jackpot): the whole share goes back to the jackpot for the next winning week — never to 50pick. Both need The decision in words.
Refused when
The share is not held; the wallet no longer holds the share (forfeit); the decision is too short or too long.
If refused
Read the account's wallet with Finance before deciding again.

B16 · Pay a winner who cannot sign in

Who
Compliance, when the holder asks to be paid.
Where
Integrity → Winners who cannot sign in: each share, not held, owed to a winner whose account is self-excluded, closed or suspended, with what is paid and what is left, until it is paid in full. (A suspended or closed winner's share is held at the settlement; it appears here once released under Held shares.) When the holder asks, press Pay…, check The holder's mobile-money network (the network of their last deposit is pre-selected), write How and when the holder asked to be paid, type PAY SHARE and press Pay to the registered number. The money goes only to the number registered on the account, at the usual withdrawal fee and within the most one withdrawal may send: a larger share is paid in equal parts that add up to it exactly, one press each. The account stays self-excluded, closed or suspended, and nothing else leaves the wallet. The answer says paid, being sent (the row stays until it lands), or refused by the mobile-money service — the money is back in the wallet: press again.
Refused when
The holder has never verified their identity or has no confirmed email address (the withdrawal rules do not change for this); the share is held or forfeited; the wallet carries another hold, or holds less than the payment; a payment of the share is still being sent; after its fee the payment is below the smallest amount mobile money sends; withdrawals are paused, on every network or on that one; the account can sign in (its holder withdraws the share).
If refused
The row says why before you press. A holder who never verified their identity or confirmed an email address cannot be paid this way: refer them to the Owner. Decide another hold on its own control, or wait for the payment being sent, then pay again.

B17 · Reach every winner

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Winners not yet reached: recent winners whom no notice is known to have reached, with what was sent. Call them on the number on their account, then Reached by phone…, say how they were reached, type REACHED, Record the call. A winner leaves the list once they open the result in the app, their SMS is delivered, or a call is recorded. A day after the settlement the row reads past 24 h, and Trading and the Owner are alerted.
Refused when
The week is not settled; the account holds no winning ticket of that week; the note is too short; the record could not be written (the winner stays listed).
If refused
Try again.

B18 · Letters not known to have gone

Who
Compliance.
Where
Integrity → Letters not known to have gone: a week's win or refund letters that the email provider accepted but never confirmed as sent. Check the provider's activity, then Checked…, say what was checked, type CHECKED, Record the check: those letters are closed for good and never sent again.
Refused when
The week's letters are being sent; the note is too short.
If refused
Wait until the sending finishes; try again.

B19 · Void a whole week

Who
The platform, by itself, once the objection window closes; Compliance, if it has not.
Where
Integrity → Weeks that void whole: a week whose sealed record makes the void certain and whose objection window has closed. Void (the button names the week), type VOID, Void and refund: every live ticket is refunded in full, no fee is taken, and the jackpot carried is untouched. Search by week code, filter Line, choose Sort by (Week, Tickets to refund) and Order, press Apply.
Refused when
The void is not final on the sealed record; the window is still open; an objection stands; the refunds did not land — the week stays voided and appears under Refunds owed.
If refused
Wait for the window or the objection rulings. Refunds owed are retried by the platform until they land; tell an administrator.

B20 · Download a week's evidence

Who
Compliance, the Auditor, the Owner.
Where
Evidence: every week that has opened, newest first. Find a week by its code, filter State (All states, or one state) and Line (Both lines, Public, Drive), Apply; sort by Week, State, Sales closed (EAT) or Tickets. Once a week settles or voids, Pack (JSON) and Tickets (CSV); every download is recorded. The pack holds the frozen rules, the fifteen questions, every seal and void with its evidence and officer, the week's audit record, the objections, the commitment checked again, the settlement's ledger lines (or the void's refunds) and any forfeits, the guarantee's movements, the winners and the connected-accounts report. Tickets (CSV) holds every ticket; the pack names it by its count and fingerprint, so the two belong together. Accounts appear by id only.
Refused when
The week is not final (Ready once it settles or voids.); your role cannot read the pack; the download could not be recorded (nothing is handed over).
If refused
Wait until the week settles or voids, or try again in a moment.

B21 · Look up a ticket for a player

Who
Support. Compliance reads the page.
Where
Ticket lookup → Ticket serial and Phone number on the account → Look up. One ticket per request; every lookup is recorded. The answer shows the ticket's state, price, results, share or refund, account and rules version — and its fifteen answers once the week is final. Under What the desk says, the scripts carry the coming weeks' rules and today's withdrawal limit and fee; the script beside a looked-up ticket carries its own week's. Read them as written, and never suggest answers.
Refused when
The serial and the number do not match one ticket; too many lookups in a short time; the lookup's record could not be written (nothing is shown).
If refused
Check the serial (MLN-…) and the registered number; wait a moment and try again.

B22 · The money reports

Who
Finance; Compliance and the Auditor read.
Where
Pools → Rollover trail: every settled week — search Find a week by code, filter the line, sort by Week, Settled (newest first), Sales, Winners, Paid or Carried on. Tax report → the Millionea pill, shown when the period holds a Millionea week: Jackpot brought forward, House floor top-up, Carried to the jackpot, and Millionea's commission — the fee its weeks booked at payout. Reports → Millionea · weekly jackpot. House book → Millionea jackpot carried for players, Millionea guarantee (Owner capital, committed), Millionea tickets in weeks not yet settled, Jackpot guarantee paid.
Refused when
The monthly report is not built while Millionea's weeks cannot be read. Elsewhere a figure that cannot be read is never shown as 0: Pools says Cannot be read, Reports leaves the jackpot lines out and says so, and the tax report lists the week as an exception.
If refused
Never file a figure that was not read: tell an administrator, then try again.

B23 · Stop Millionea

Who
The Owner.
Where
Pools → Owner → Stop the product: The final week (Find the final week by code when there are many) and Why Millionea is stopping, then Make it the final week, type SUNSET MILLIONEA, Make it final. Three ways: a draft named a full week before it opens; the week on sale, at once; while a week's results are being decided and none is on sale, the next week to open, at shorter notice. With no week to offer, the card names the next act.
Refused when
A week whose sales have closed (it settles under its own rules); a draft opening in less than a full week, unless it is that next week; a later week already opened; a final week already standing; the reason is too short or too long.
If refused
Follow the next act the refusal names.

A final week never opens before its announced time. Pausing sales, voiding a week and stopping Millionea are three different acts.

Part 3 · What everything means

Weeks and questions

Week codeMLN-year-Wweek: the year, and the week's number in it.
Public · DriveThe week's line: on sale to players, or a private line that is not in use — a drive week sells to no one.
DraftBeing written; nothing is on sale.
SellingOpen: tickets can be bought.
ClosedSales have closed; sealing has not started.
SealingFrom the first seal until the week settles or voids: the seals, the objection window, the release.
SettledPaid out, or rolled over. Final.
VoidedVoided whole; every ticket refunded in full.
Final (beside a code)The final week: Millionea ends after it.
Results: N of 15 sealed · needs NSealed so far, and how many more the settlement waits for.
Results: Final · window to …The graded set is final; the objection window ends then.
Voids wholeMore mains voided than usable reserves: the week voids and refunds.
Main · reserve (Akiba)M1–M10 are graded; A1–A5 stand in, in order, for mains that void.
Graded setThe ten questions a ticket is graded on. The published rules call them the counted questions.
SealAn officer's recorded result for one question — YES, NO or VOID — with its evidence.
Not yetNo result chosen for that question.
void proposedIts void deadline passed unsealed: seal its result if the source gave it by the deadline, otherwise the void.
awaits a second officerA two-officer seal not yet confirmed; it does not count yet.
Void deadlineThe latest time a question's event may be played or read; printed on the question.
Final deadlineThe latest any void deadline may fall, in the week after the close.
Objection windowOpens when the graded set is final, for the hours the week froze, and never closes before the time of day the rules set. Holders may object while it is open, and nothing is paid. A correction restarts it; so does the first upheld ruling that changes the graded set. The published rules call it the check window.
Objection: Open · settlement frozen · Upheld · RejectedWaiting for a ruling, and holding the week's settlement; ruled for the holder; ruled against.
AttestationA second reader's record that the three languages agree. An edit clears it.
Fingerprint · root · commitmentA fingerprint is a short code computed from a record: any change to the record changes it. The question set's fingerprint is published at the open; the root, one fingerprint over every ticket standing at the close, is published with the count and the time. Together they are the commitment: proof that nothing changed later.
RegisterThe public record of each settled or voided week: questions, results, voids, money.

Money

JackpotWhat a winning week shares: the floor, or the carry-in plus the week's sales, whichever is larger.
FloorThe smallest jackpot a winning week pays, counted before the fee.
Fee at payoutThe week's percentage of the jackpot, taken only when a ticket wins; TRA and GBT come out of it.
ShareOne winning ticket's part of the jackpot after the fee; equal for every winning ticket.
Rounding carriedThe few shillings an equal split leaves over: they join the next jackpot as far as the week's own money covers them; the rest stays with the guarantee. Shown on the register.
Jackpot carriedPlayers' money from weeks nobody won, waiting for the next winning week.
GuaranteeOwner capital backing the floor; beside it, what the weeks already open need.
Top-upWhat the guarantee adds to a winning week to reach the floor.
Payout floatThe money winners withdraw from; beside it, the payouts already prepared.
HeadroomWhat the payout float keeps back for other withdrawals.
Drive jackpotThe drive line's own jackpot; the line is not in use.
SalesLive tickets × the week's ticket price.
Carry inThe jackpot a week claimed when the week before it became final; Not yet until then.
CoverageHow many of the 1,024 ways to answer the ten mains some live ticket holds.
Worst top-upThe most the week could still draw from the guarantee.
SettlementThe prepared figures and the release; Waiting on the objection window. or Not ready. before then.
Winners · Fee · Top-up · PaidA settled week's winning tickets, fee, guarantee top-up and total paid.
Carried onWhat the settled week left in the jackpot for the next.
Levy on own top-upThe TRA and GBT levies on the fee the guarantee's own top-up created, paid from 50pick's money.
Cannot be readThe figure is unknown — never zero.

Only campaigns named this way are checked; others are not judged in this release.
