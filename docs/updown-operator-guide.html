Up & Down — Operator Guide

For 50pick staff who run the Up & Down game. No technical knowledge needed. Every picture is the real production screen, photographed on 4 August 2026. ⛔ This replaces every earlier version — the rules below are new and the old guide contradicts them.

THE RULES WE SET TODAY — read this page before you touch anything

Gold only trades on certain days. Gold (and any currency pair) is shut every weekend: it opens Monday 01:00 EAT and closes Saturday 00:00 EAT. The platform refuses to open or settle a gold round while the market is shut — you will see “market shut” on the chain, and that is correct, not a fault. Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL) trades 24 hours, 7 days, including weekends and public holidays.
Gold cannot run shorter than 15 minutes. The dropdown greys 3, 5 and 10 minutes for gold and tells you why. Gold’s own price feed disagrees with itself by up to $0.87 at a single instant, which is more than gold typically moves in five minutes — a short gold round would be decided by which reading arrived, not by the market. Crypto can run every length: 3, 5, 10, 15, 30, 60 minutes.
Betting stops before the round ends. The last 20% of every round takes no bets (never less than 30 seconds). A 5-minute round shuts betting with 60 seconds left. Why: near the close the result is almost already known. Without this, someone watching the price could bet on an outcome they can see coming, and take money off the players who bet honestly at the start. The player’s card says so plainly, and the countdown changes from “betting closes in” to “result in”.
The winning band is the smallest possible step — not a percentage. The recommended setting is “Smallest possible”, which uses the asset’s own minimum move: $0.02 on Bitcoin, $0.40 on gold. About 99 rounds in 100 then pay a winner. Why: we measured it. At 0.50% (the old default) BTC had to move ±$316 inside five minutes — it refunded 5 real rounds out of 5. A wide band does not make more money; it makes no money, because a refunded round earns no fee.
One account may only take one side of a round. A player cannot bet UP and DOWN on the same round. Why: betting both sides is not a bet — it is a way to move money around while paying only the fee, and on a lopsided pool it can even be profitable regardless of the result.
A round that closes late still settles — it no longer refunds. Prices are now read from a dated one-minute bar, so the price at 13:52 is still the price at 13:52 an hour later. If a close is delayed we go back and read the right minute, up to 24 hours. Before today a late close refunded everyone even though nothing had failed.
Every refund tells the player its real reason. Six different reasons, each in English, Swahili and Chinese, on the card, the round page, the settlement proof, the push and the inbox. Never say “the price did not move” about a refund that happened for another reason — that sentence used to appear on a refund caused by nobody taking the other side.
Rounds are produced by hand. You press Generate round. Nothing runs automatically. Why: while we are learning the product, an operator watching is worth more than a timer nobody is watching. Do not start a chain and walk away.
If only one side bets, everybody is refunded. A round needs UP and DOWN money to have a winner. A one-sided round refunds every stake and earns nothing. This is the one thing that will disappoint players in a quiet hour. Prefer few chains with players on them over many empty ones.

DO NOT Do not run gold below 15 minutes. Do not widen the band to “look serious”. Do not add a chain nobody is playing. Those three are the only ways an operator can lose money on this game today, and all three feel harmless.

1 · What the game is

A player bets whether a price — Bitcoin, Ethereum, gold — will be higher (UP) or lower (DOWN) at the end of a short window. One window is a round.

1. Round opensWe read the price from the last finished minute and freeze it. That is the opening price.
2. Players betUP or DOWN, until betting shuts in the last 20%.
3. Round closesWe read the price for the closing minute — the exact same way.
4. We payUP wins, DOWN wins, or nobody wins and everyone gets their stake back.

THE ONE IDEA TO UNDERSTAND We settle against a dated one-minute bar, not “the price now”. Every minute of the day has a permanent record: its opening price. “The price at 13:52” means “the opening price of the bar labelled 13:52”. That number never changes, so we can always go back and check it — which is why a delayed round can still be settled honestly instead of refunded, and why nobody can argue about which price we used.

2 · How Twelve Data works in our favour

Twelve Data is the company that sells us prices. Three things about it are genuinely to our advantage, and they are the reason the game is built the way it is.

What it gives usWhy that is in our favour
The past is readable
a dated bar, not a live tick		 A live price can only answer “what is it now”. Miss the instant and the number is gone forever — so a delay had to refund. A dated bar is the same number six hours later, so a late close settles correctly. It also means a player who disputes a result can be shown the exact minute we used.
A whole window costs one credit
1–5000 minutes, same price		 One request can fetch a thousand minutes for the price of one. Six round lengths on one asset need about 672 separate price readings a day against a plan of roughly 800 — which would be impossible one at a time. Fetching a window instead collapses that to 24–96. This is what makes 3-minute rounds affordable at all.
Real-time crypto, forex and US shares Our plan gives live prices for crypto, currencies and metals. That covers every asset we want. ⛔ It does not cover stock indices (the S&P 500 returns nothing), and shares outside the USA are end-of-day only — a once-a-day price cannot settle a 5-minute round, so no non-US share may ever be an Up & Down asset.

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED A minute’s bar is published a few seconds after that minute ends — about 10 seconds for BTC, ETH and gold, and about 60 seconds for Solana. That is why Generate round takes the opening price from the last finished minute, and why the platform waits up to two minutes for a bar before it treats it as missing. You do not have to do anything about this; it is why generation sometimes takes a few seconds to answer.

HOW MUCH IS “A CHANGE”? Any movement of one band or more decides the round. On Bitcoin that is 2 cents — a normal 3-minute Bitcoin move is hundreds of times that, which is why ~99% of rounds pay. 4 · The formulas — exactly how a round resolves, and why

Nothing here is approximate. These are the actual calculations, and every number in the worked examples is from a real production round settled on 4 August 2026.

4.1 · The two targets, frozen when the round opens

tick = 0.01 (the asset's smallest price step, 2 decimals) band = the LARGER of: open x margin% ... the percentage, if you set one minMoveTicks x tick ... the asset's minimum move UP target = opening price + band DOWN target = opening price - band

With the recommended Smallest possible setting the percentage is 0, so the band is always the asset’s minimum move. Both targets are written onto the round at open and never recalculated — so changing a setting later cannot alter a round players already bet on.

Worked example — the real round udr_a1adea90..., BTC 5m opening price 63,856.00 band 2 ticks x 0.01 = 0.02 UP target 63,856.02 DOWN target 63,855.98

4.2 · The outcome

closing price >= UP target -> UP wins closing price <= DOWN target -> DOWN wins anything in between -> VOID: every stake refunded in full, no fee taken
Same round closing price 63,832.00 which is <= 63,855.98 -> DOWN WINS

4.3 · The money

Up & Down is a pool game. Everyone’s stake goes into one pot; the winning side shares the pot in proportion to what each of them staked. We take a commission of 13%, but never more than one third of the smaller side.

pool = UP money + DOWN money smaller = whichever side has less money on it commission = 13% x pool ceiling = 33.33% x smaller fee = the SMALLER of commission and ceiling net pool = pool - fee one winner's payout = round( their stake / winning side's total x net pool )

WHY THE CEILING EXISTS It guarantees a winner can never receive less than they staked. Because the fee can never exceed a third of the smaller side, the net pool is always at least as large as the winning side — so every winner gets back at least their stake. The platform refuses to settle a round where that is not true, rather than pay a “winner” less than they put in. You will never have to explain that to a player, because it cannot happen.

Worked example A — a balanced round (the real one) UP 500 + DOWN 500 pool 1,000 smaller 500 commission 13% x 1,000 = 130 ceiling 33.33% x 500 = 166.67 fee min(130, 166.67) = 130 net pool 870 DOWN won. One winner staked 500 of the 500 on DOWN: payout = 500/500 x 870 = 870 the platform keeps 130 Worked example B — a lopsided round, showing the ceiling bite UP 900 + DOWN 100 pool 1,000 smaller 100 commission 13% x 1,000 = 130 ceiling 33.33% x 100 = 33.33 fee min(130, 33.33) = 33.33 CAPPED net pool 966.67 If DOWN wins, its single 100 backer receives 966 - nearly ten times the stake. If UP wins, the 900 receives 966. Either way, above the stake.

4.4 · When betting shuts

lead = the LARGER of ( 20% of the round , 30 seconds ) betting shuts at = closing time - lead

At that moment the player’s buttons grey out, the card explains why, and the estimate “× 1.4 est.” is replaced by an exact figure — because once the pool is frozen we know precisely what each side would receive.

4.5 · Which round lengths are allowed, and why only those six

a length is allowed only if it divides evenly into 1440 (the minutes in a day) 3 · 5 · 10 · 15 · 30 · 60 -> allowed 4 · 7 · 45 -> refused

A length that divides the day makes every round start on a clean clock mark, the same marks every day. It also means several lengths share boundaries, so one price reading serves many rounds — the credit saving described in section 2.

4.6 · If a price cannot be read

a "not published yet" answer within 120 seconds -> free, we simply retry retries at 15s, 45s, then 120s, up to 4 real attempts still nothing after 390 seconds -> VOID, everyone refunded a delayed close, up to 24 hours later -> we read the correct minute and SETTLE

You do not manage any of this. It is here so that when you see “confirming price” for a minute or two, you know it is the system retrying on purpose and not something you should touch.

Up & Down is a separate game from the normal polls, with its own section near the bottom of the sidebar: UP & DOWN · JUU NA CHINI. It has three screens — Overview (what you trade and the chains that produce rounds), AI proposals, and Rounds (every round that has run).

⚠️ You will not find Up & Down rounds under Markets → Curation queue. That section is long-form polls only.

7.2 · The chain row, and the Generate button

ControlWhat it does · when to use it
Generate round Creates one round, right now, and opens it for betting. This is your main button. It reads the opening price from the last finished minute; if it cannot read a price it refuses and creates nothing — no round, no stakes, nothing to unwind.

The Edit panel (it opens inside the chain’s own row, so it is narrow and the fields stack). The band is the same dropdown as the Add form — you cannot type a number here either, and for the same reason.

Inherit follows the product setting, so changing that one setting changes every chain that inherits it. The other three choices override this chain alone.

⚠️ Changes affect future rounds only. A round already open keeps the band it froze at open — otherwise the rule a player bet under could be changed after they had bet.

8 · Your first live round — the ten-minute walkthrough

  1. Up & Down → Overview. Confirm BTC is enabled and reads 2 dp · min 2.
  2. + Add chain → Asset ① BTC · Bitcoin → Duration ① 5 min → Winning band Smallest possible → stakes blank → Add chain. It appears STOPPED. That is correct.
  3. Check MARKET reads open and MARGIN reads ±0.02 ·min move.
  4. Press Generate round. Wait for the row to show a round. If it refuses with “could not read a price”, wait fifteen seconds and press it again — you were inside the few seconds before the minute’s bar was published.
  5. Open 50pick.tz/updown in another tab as a player. Your round is on the 5 min tab — the board opens on 3 min by default, so a 5-minute round is not missing, you are on the wrong tab.
  6. Place a small bet on UP. Watch VOL grow. From a second account bet DOWN — you need both sides or the round refunds.
  7. With 60 seconds left, watch the buttons grey out, the wording change from “betting closes in” to “result in”, and the estimate become an exact figure.
  8. At the close the card shows the result. Check Rounds: the outcome, the stake, the players and the settlement time.
  9. Check the winner’s wallet went up by the amount the card promised. It will agree to the shilling.

THIS EXACT WALKTHROUGH WAS DONE ON PRODUCTION BTC 5-minute round, real money on both sides, opening 63,856.00 → closing 63,832.00 → DOWN. The UP stake of 500 paid 0; the DOWN stake of 500 paid 870; the winner’s wallet went from 24,400 to 25,270. Pool 1,000, fee 130. Every figure matched.

9 · Refunds — the six reasons, and what to tell a player

A refund is not a loss and not an error. The player gets their whole stake back and we take no fee. But a player who is refunded always wants to know why, and the reason is on their screen already — in English, Swahili or Chinese, whichever they use. Read it before answering.

Reason shownWhat actually happenedWhat you say
Price did not move enough
no-move		 The close landed between the two targets. Rare on the recommended band. “The price finished inside the band, so neither side won. Your full stake is back.”
Nobody took the other side
unmatched		 Only UP or only DOWN had money. There was no pool to pay from. “Nobody bet against you, so there was nothing to win and nothing to lose. Your stake is back in full.” ⛔ Never call this “the price did not move” — it used to say that, and it was wrong.
We could not read the price
source-failed		 The provider did not answer in time. Our problem, not theirs. “We could not confirm the closing price, so we refunded everyone rather than guess.”
Market was shut
market-closed		 A gold round hit the weekend. “Gold does not trade at weekends, so the round could not be settled and everyone was refunded.”
Cancelled by us
operator		 Staff voided it deliberately. The reason you typed is on the record permanently. Whatever the truth is. Always give a real reason when you void.
Round expired
abandoned		 No price could be read within the deadline at all. “We ran out of time to confirm a price, so every stake was returned.”

NEVER Never announce a price we have not confirmed. If a screen shows a dash, that is the honest answer. Do not fill it in from another website — the round did not settle on that website and the player was never told it would.

10 · Your daily check — five minutes

  1. Rounds — are recent rounds resolving UP/DOWN rather than a run of refunds?
  2. PAID A WINNER · 7D on each chain. On the recommended band this should be high. A low figure with reason source-failed is our problem — tell the technical team. A low figure with no-move means the band is too wide for that asset and length.
  3. MARKET column — gold reads shut at the weekend. Expected, not a fault.
  4. Both sides betting? A chain whose rounds keep refunding as unmatched has no audience. Stop it and put the players on one busier chain instead.
  5. Money — staked and paid out look sensible for the day.

11 · When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
Generate says “could not read a price” You pressed it in the few seconds before the minute’s bar was published. Nothing was created. Wait fifteen seconds and press it again. If it fails three times, tell the technical team.
Gold chain reads market shut It is between Saturday 00:00 and Monday 01:00 EAT. Nothing. Correct behaviour. Run crypto over the weekend.
Rounds keep refunding unmatched Only one side is betting. Fewer chains, more players per chain. This is a liquidity problem, not a fault.
Rounds keep refunding no-move The band is too wide for that asset over that length. Edit the chain → band → Smallest possible. Future rounds only.
“Confirming price” for a minute or two The retry ladder, working as designed. Wait. If it never confirms, the round refunds by itself.
A round is stuck holding money Something went wrong for that one round. Rounds → Void, with a real reason. Every stake is refunded in full.
You cannot change an asset’s price source A round that captured the old source is still unresolved. Correct behaviour — those players were told the old source. Let them finish first.
A button reads 🔒 ACCOUNTING ONLY Your role cannot use that control. Ask the owner. Do not look for a way around it.
