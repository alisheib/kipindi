# §B — PURGE A CHAIN AND ITS HISTORY

Ali's instruction: a real delete, not just archive — with a confirmation, a warning from our
kit stating what it would cost, a progress bar and a loader, done cleanly.

## B0 · Read this before designing anything

**Archive already exists and stays.** This is the *second*, heavier door — not a replacement.
Default stays Archive; Purge is the exception.

**The cascade does NOT do what "delete all history" means.** Verified against the schema:

- `UpDownChain → UpDownRound` is `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Deleting the chain deletes every round.
- `UpDownRound → PredictionMarket` is **also** CASCADE but declared **on the round side**, so
  it fires market→round, *not* round→market. **Deleting a round does not delete its market.**
- So today's cascade destroys the price story and leaves every `PredictionMarket`, `Position`,
  `Comment`, `Watchlist` and `MarketSnapshot` fully alive and unreachable from `/admin/updown`.
  **That is exactly the 2026-08-04 incident** — ~2,515 rounds cleared, 1,915 phantom
  "failures", and `scripts/live/ops/e63-window.cjs` written to diagnose it.
- ⛔ `Objection → PredictionMarket` is **RESTRICT**. A market carrying an objection cannot be
  deleted at all; the FK throws. Check first or the job dies mid-batch.
- ⛔ `LedgerEntry.marketId`, `HousePoolLedger.marketId` and `Transaction.positionId` are
  **loose strings with no FK**. They survive any delete and dangle. Ledger reconciliation
  breaks silently.
- ✅ `UpDownObservation` has **no FK to a round or chain** — it is keyed
  `@@unique([assetId, boundaryAt])` and deliberately **shared by the 5/15/30-min chains**.
  A chain purge must touch **zero** observations. ⛔ Never copy
  `scripts/ops-updown-reset-games.mts:139` (`deleteMany({})`) — unscoped, it steals the
  readings other durations still need, and re-observing a confirmed boundary is forbidden.
- ⛔ **`AuditLog` is never touched.** It is HMAC-chained with `@@unique([prevHash])`; deleting
  one row breaks `verifyChain()` permanently and destroys the evidence for every *other*
  ceremony. The purge **writes** audit rows and removes none.

**Retention is 7 years** — POCA Cap 423 §16 + TRA Income Tax Act §80. The retention engine
(`src/lib/server/retention.ts`) prunes only Notification / Otp / AIPoll payloads and its own
design rule is that it "names the classes it touches and cannot reach a fourth". Money, audit
and KYC are "deliberately never deleted". **So the purge must refuse rounds still inside the
retention window** unless Ali overrides in writing, and must record the statutory basis and
trigger date in its audit row.

## B1 · Where it lives — and why not on `/admin/updown`

Gate it on **`compliance`** (`CONTROL_DOMAIN.purgeChainHistory: "compliance"`). Precedent:
`emergencyVoidMarket` is the one existing control pushed up to `compliance` purely because it
is destructive and moves money. ⛔ Not `accounting` — FINANCE is the role whose own work the
settlement record evidences, and letting it erase that record is the self-review the AML gate
already forbids.

⚠️ **`/admin/updown` is a `trading` route.** A `compliance` control on a `trading` route is
Owner-only-in-practice and logs every legitimate click as `privilege_escalation_blocked` —
that is the documented E-18/E-23 failure, and `voidUpDownRound` had to be corrected within the
hour for it. So: host the ceremony on **`/admin/retention`** (already `compliance`), and give
the Archived-chains row on `/admin/updown` a **link** — "Purge history →" pointing at
`/admin/retention?purge=chain:<id>` — not the control itself.

## B2 · Preconditions (server-side, before anything renders)

Refuse, naming the count and the remedy, unless **all** hold:

1. Chain state is `ARCHIVED`. Archive is the required prior step, not an alternative.
2. Every round is resolved **and** settled (or voided and refunded). No money in flight.
3. No market in the set carries an `Objection` (RESTRICT would throw mid-job).
4. ⭐ **No retention check, and NO OVERRIDE MECHANISM — decided.** Under §B8 the statutory
   record (market, positions, transactions, ledger, audit) is never destroyed, and the round's
   settlement evidence is not destroyed either — §B4 relocates it into a signed, hash-anchored
   pack before anything is deleted. So the 7-year mandate is satisfied *by construction*, at
   any age, and there is nothing to override. ⛔ Do not build an override flag: a feature with
   no override has no override to abuse, and an override on this control is the one thing a
   regulator would ask to see the usage log for.

## B7 · The progress bar

⚠️ **There is no precedent for this in the repo** — every admin job today is a single blocking
server action behind an indeterminate spinner. You are building the first one. Do it properly:

- A **job row** (`chainId`, `state`, `phase`, `total`, `done`, `startedAt`, `finishedAt`,
  `error`, `packHash`, `officerA`, `officerB`). ⚠️ Needs a migration — `prisma/` is Session M's
  lane, so **write the migration and flag it to M rather than applying it**.
- The action creates the job and returns immediately. A batched worker deletes in chunks
  (~200 rounds per transaction), updating `done` after each batch.
- The client polls the job row (`RefreshPoller` exists) and draws a **determinate** bar:
  `done / total`, elapsed, and the phase — `exporting → deleting → verifying → done`.
- **Resumable by construction**: progress is a DB row, so a crash resumes instead of
  restarting. Each batch is its own transaction.
- Bar and spinner come from the kit. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (every keyframe in this
  repo ships a branch — don't be the first that doesn't).

## B8 · What actually gets deleted — DECIDED, build exactly this

⭐ **REDACT, DON'T DESTROY.** This is not a new idea — it is the idiom this platform already
uses everywhere it must remove data without losing provability: the retention engine **blanks
AIPoll payload columns** rather than deleting rows, and DSAR erasure **anonymises** rather
than destroys betting records. A purge follows the same law.

**DELETE** (the price story and the player-facing chaff — no money meaning):
- `UpDownRound` — openPrice/closePrice/targets/captured source/outcome/observation pointers
- `Comment`, `Watchlist`, `MarketSnapshot` for those markets

**REDACT TO A TOMBSTONE — never delete:**
- `PredictionMarket` survives. Blank the titles and resolution criterion; **keep** pools,
  `feeSnapshot`, `resolvedOutcome`, `settledAt`. Stamp `purgedAt`, `purgedBy`, `purgeReason`.

**NEVER TOUCH:**
- `Position`, `Transaction`, `LedgerEntry`, `HousePoolLedger`, `AuditLog`, `UpDownObservation`

### Why this is the right answer and not a compromise

Every hazard in §B0 dissolves at once, by construction rather than by care:

- **Nothing dangles.** `LedgerEntry.marketId`, `HousePoolLedger.marketId` and
  `Transaction.positionId` are loose strings with no FK — the reason they were a hazard is
  that their target could vanish. It no longer can.
- **`Objection`'s RESTRICT never fires**, because no market is ever deleted. The precondition
  check for it becomes unnecessary — keep it as a tripwire, but it can no longer block a job.
- **`test:trial-balance` still balances and every statutory read is unchanged.** GGR, NGR,
  settlement fees and the regulator packs all read the market row, which is still there.
  ⛔ No adjusting entry, no re-baselining — a licensed operator does not rewrite its books to
  tidy a console.

  🔴 **AND THIS IS THE DECIDING ARGUMENT, PROVEN ON PRODUCTION ON 2026-08-28 — the same day
  this spec was written.** A teardown script refunded open positions and then deleted the
  markets; the positions cascade-deleted with them and **two `STAKE_DEBIT` ledger pairs were
  left standing**, so the books claimed TZS 2,000 was held in escrow for a market that no
  longer existed. `house-money.cjs` **still printed "the books balance"** — both halves of
  each pair were present and the grand total was still 0. The same run surfaced 3
  pre-existing orphaned `POOL:*` accounts nobody had noticed.
  ⛔ **A market delete at the scale of a chain purge would manufacture that defect thousands
  of times over, and every money suite would stay green across all of it.** A grand total of
  zero is not the statement *"every account means what it says"*. The tombstone is what makes
  the second statement true.
  ⭐ While you are here, add the check that was missing: **does every `POOL:*` account belong
  to a market row that still exists?** It is the guard this whole class needs, and the purge
  is the feature that makes it load-bearing.
- **`verifyChain()` still passes**, because no audit row is removed.
- **The e63 phantom-failure shape cannot recur.** That incident happened because deletion was
  *silent and unlabelled* — ops tooling counted markets-without-rounds as failures. A stamped
  `purgedAt` is the opposite of unlabelled. ⛔ Part of this work is teaching the ops/report
  reads to treat a stamped market as **purged**, not failed. Fix `e63-window.cjs`'s question
  at the source.
- **The player sees it gone.** No round, no comments, no watchlist entry, blanked titles.
  Which is what "delete it" means from the console.
