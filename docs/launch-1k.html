Railway's last four bills were $5.00, $11.15, $25.42 and $17.38 so far this period. The cost is rising because the platform is doing more work, not because anything is wrong.

Two facts that decide where the money goes.
The AI bill is bigger than the hosting bill — $50.75 against $31.81 — and 99.98% of it does not depend on customer numbers. It is the cost of creating and settling markets on a clock. Going from 104 customers to 1,000 will barely change it.
Memory is 87% of the 50pick hosting bill — $20.60 of $23.57. The application uses almost no processing power and holds 4.7 GB of memory. Capping that is the cheapest saving we have.

What is already limited, and what is not

RiskLimit in place todayExposure
AI overspend $100 per cycle. Currently $71.48 spent of $100. The limit fails open. If the meter itself errors, spending continues unmetered. This is the largest uncontrolled line.
SMS pumping 5 codes then 1 per 2 minutes per phone; 3 sign-ups per hour per phone; 10 new phones per address per 20 minutes. Bounded. A determined attacker across ten addresses reaches roughly 10,000 messages a day — about $100/day — and is visible immediately.
Traffic under attack Railway caps 10,000 requests per second per host. Unlimited cost. Traffic is billed per gigabyte and there is no firewall in front of the site. This is the only line with no ceiling.
Database disk Billed on space used; can grow to 1 TB. $0.15 per GB per month. A full disk stops the platform accepting bets until resized.
Runaway scaling None. Railway bills what is consumed. Set a spending limit on the Railway account. It is not set today.
Three spending limits worth setting before launch, all free: a hard spend cap on the Railway account; an alert when the AI cycle passes 80% of $100; and Cloudflare in front of the site, which removes the only line that has no ceiling. Together these cap the Max scenario at roughly $600 instead of $1,766.

Two figures in our own internal notes were wrong, both in our favour. The code and documentation state that the database allows 100 simultaneous connections and that Redis is switched off in production. Measured live: the database allows 500, and Redis is connected and working across containers. Several "we cannot scale" conclusions written earlier this year rested on those two stale figures and no longer hold.

Money safety — built, tested, and not to be rebuilt

  • Maintenance mode pauses new bets and deposits while leaving withdrawals open, so customer funds are never trapped by an operational decision.
  • Payment kill switches per mobile network, separately for money in and money out.
  • Deposits are settled from a signed re-query, not the incoming message, exactly once, defended against a tampered amount, and recovered by a 15-second poller and a 5-minute sweep even if the provider's confirmation never arrives.
  • A dead Redis cannot break a bet — proven by a test that points the platform at a dead port and asserts betting still succeeds.
  • A retry without an idempotency key is refused, so a retry can never become a second bet.
  • 200 simultaneous bets on one market: zero shillings lost or created.
  • The Up & Down safety net is independent of the game's own off switch — turning the game off must not disable the thing that returns money already staked in it.
  • Backups are verified by actually restoring them nightly into a scratch database and re-checking the money totals and the audit chain — not by confirming a file was written.
