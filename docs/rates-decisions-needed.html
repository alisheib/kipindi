2 · Why two-sided betting cannot clear a bonus

How bonuses work. Bonus money cannot be withdrawn straight away. The player must bet it first — currently five times the amount granted, within 30 days. Once they have bet that much, the bonus becomes real, withdrawable cash.

Now that a player may back both sides of the same round, that requirement could be met without taking any risk at all:

What the rule prevents — a 10,000 bonus, cleared without a single prediction

We grant Amina a bonus of10,000
She must bet 5 × that before withdrawing50,000
She bets 25,000 on UP and 25,000 on DOWN of the same round50,000 — requirement met
One side wins, one loses. She receives back46,750
Her total cost — just our fee3,250
Her net gain on a 10,000 bonus6,750

She never predicted anything. Without this rule, every bonus we issue would be worth roughly two-thirds of its face value in cash on the day it is issued, to anyone who knows the trick.

The rule, precisely. When a player holds bets on both sides of the same market, only one side counts toward a bonus betting requirement. Everything else about the bet is normal: it is placed, it can win, it counts toward leaderboards and activity like any other bet. This is the only place where two-sided betting is treated differently, and only because the money at the end of it is ours.

Nothing is affected today. Production currently holds zero bonus grants and zero bonus balance. This rule takes effect before the first grant is issued — including for the VIP rewards scheme, which will run on this same machinery.

What the player is told

The player must never discover this after the fact. When someone holding a bonus takes the opposite side of a market they are already on, they get a clear warning before confirming — a warning, not an error, and the bet still goes ahead if they want it:

This bet won't count toward your bonus.
You already have money on the other side of this market. Only one side counts toward the TZS 32,000 you still need to bet before your bonus can be withdrawn.
Place bet anyway · Cancel

The same rule is written into the bonus terms shown when a bonus is granted, the player rules, the wallet screen where the remaining requirement is displayed, and the administrator guide — in the player's own language, in all three languages the app supports.

4 · Maswali Millionea — the third product (decided 16 October 2026)

A weekly jackpot, on rules of its own. It is not pari-mutuel: a player buys a ticket at a fixed price and answers fifteen YES/NO questions, and every ticket with all ten graded answers right shares the week’s jackpot equally. Decided by the Owner on 16 October 2026 and written into the platform rules the next day. Not on sale yet: it ships switched off for every player, and no week can open until the Owner has recorded the licence basis and funded the guarantee. Every figure below is an administrator setting; each week freezes its own at its open, and a change applies from the next week.

RuleWhat it meansDecided by
A fixed ticket TZS 1,000 a ticket, from the real balance only — bonus money never buys one. Owner, 16 Oct
Fifteen answers, ten that count Ten main questions and five reserves, in a fixed order. A ticket wins only on 10 of 10. A main question that voids is replaced by the first unused reserve; if more mains void than there are usable reserves, the whole week voids and every ticket is refunded in full. Owner, 16 Oct
The week Sales open Monday 00:00 and close Friday 18:00 EAT, always at least 30 minutes before the first of the fifteen events. After the close nothing can be bought or cancelled. Owner, 16 Oct
A floor under every winning week A week with a winner pays a jackpot of at least TZS 1,000,000, counted before our fee. When the week’s own money falls short, the guarantee — Owner capital — tops it up. Owner, 16 Oct
Our fee: 13% of the jackpot, only when someone wins 13% of the jackpot, taken only in a week with at least one 10 of 10 ticket. A week nobody wins is charged nothing. TRA 10% and GBT 5% come out of our fee, as on the other games. Owner, 16 Oct
Equal shares Every winning ticket gets the same share of what is left after the fee, rounded down to the shilling. The few shillings the split cannot share join the next jackpot as far as the week's own money covers them; the rest stays with the guarantee, whose top-up covers only the fee and the shares — so in a week the guarantee tops up, the whole remainder stays with it. There is no minimum share. Owner, 16 Oct
Nobody wins: everything rolls The whole week’s sales roll into the next jackpot. Real money only — a top-up never rolls — and there is no cap. Owner, 16 Oct
10 tickets per account per week The one exception to “as many positions as they want”. Owner, 16 Oct
Cancellation The platform’s free cancellation, unchanged: within 5 minutes of buying, a full refund; a ticket bought in the last 5 minutes before the close cannot be cancelled. There is no paid exit. Owner, 16 Oct
Who may buy Staff, and accounts 50pick operates, are refused. Every responsible-gambling limit applies. Owner, 16 Oct
Results One officer seals each question from its named source, with evidence — never the AI. A question not played and settled within 48 hours of its start voids. A 24-hour check window opens when the results are final, and never closes before 12:00 EAT. Owner, 16 Oct
Stopping the product Three cases: a week not yet on sale is named the final week at least one full week before its sales open (named on the hub and the home card from then on, and never opened early); a stop ordered while a week is on sale makes that week the final one; a stop ordered while no week is on sale and a week's results are being decided cannot take that week (it settles under its own rules); the refusal names the Owner's next act — make the next week to open (once drafted) the final one, accepted at less than a full week's notice while that week's results are being decided, its buyers told when they buy. A stop between weeks takes the first path. The final week runs normally and whatever the jackpot holds goes into it; if no ticket gets 10 of 10, the highest score shares it. Owner, 16 Oct (OD-03); after a close: build lead on delegation, 18 Oct
What Millionea does not share with the other two games. The fee comes out of the jackpot, not out of a losing side, and only in a winning week. And a winning ticket can be paid less than it cost: when very many tickets reach 10 of 10, each equal share is small. Everywhere else on the platform a winning bet is never paid less than its stake; on Millionea the player is told that it can happen, in the rules, in Help and by the assistant.
