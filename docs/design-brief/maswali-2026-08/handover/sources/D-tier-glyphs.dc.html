Why coronets: heraldic rank is the house voice; pearl count 3/2/1 mirrors 10/9/8; silhouette height falls with tier, so the three separate without colour (law 7) and without reading a count at 14px. Pearls are stroked rings, not fills — at 14px they close into dots optically while staying inside the family's fill="none" law.
Deliberately not a seal (M7 reserves seal vocabulary for wins — these glyphs also sit on loss receipts), not the existing generic zigzag crown, and never coloured: rank may be metallic elsewhere, but a tier never wears the money ink (Q5, test:gold-is-money).
