═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE TWELVE LAWS THIS PRODUCT WILL TRY TO BREAK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
A jackpot is the product category most likely to violate this rulebook, because
every jackpot convention is on this list. Each is law, not preference.

 1. NO CASINO, NO MANUFACTURED URGENCY. The countdown on a genuinely closing
    thing is the ONLY manufactured urgency permitted, and it is scoped to that
    thing. No confetti, no flashing, no streak flames, no combo meters, no
    perpetual loop. A win breathes or fades and then RESTS.
    ⛔ A number that animates upward on a timer is banned. The jackpot figure
    changes only when a ticket is actually sold, using the existing value-flash,
    which never moves layout. A persistent urgency cue in global chrome is a
    responsible-gambling finding.

 2. GOLD IS STRUCK, AND STRUCK MEANS EARNED. The gilt treatment appears ONLY
    where money was actually earned: a payout, a settled profit, a resolved seal.
    The jackpot figure is the most UNEARNED number on this platform — a prize
    nobody has won, that may roll over for months. It must NOT be gold.
    A decorative element wearing gilt is a violation, not a style choice.
    No bloom. Rays are banned. Any control wearing gilt keeps a REAL outline on
    focus (a shadow-only ring vanishes in forced-colors).

 3. UNREALISED HONESTY — A LICENCE CONDITION, NOT A PREFERENCE. Any unrealised
    figure is visibly labelled at the point it is read. Per-position potential
    payout is NOT SHOWN AT ALL before resolution. "Win TZS 20,000,000!" is a
    promised return and is a licensing problem.
    ✅ Correct form — third person, about the pool, with the rule attached:
       "Millionea pool · TZS 2,016,138 · shared by every 10/10 ticket."

 4. REAL DATA OR NOTHING — A LICENCE CONDITION. Never render a guessed,
    placeholder or zero-as-unknown number. Unknown = an em-dash PLUS a labelled
    state ("Opens Friday", "awaiting read"). A null-coalesced zero is the
    canonical bug; a skeleton number that looks like data is the same bug wearing
    a shimmer. A pending state is calm, never styled as an error.

 5. LOSSES GET THE RECEIPT, WINS GET THE SEAL — A COMPLIANCE CONDITION. The
    celebration vocabulary is EXCLUSIVE to a win. A loss is bookkeeping: calm,
    factual, final, on a plain rung, no colour ceremony, no tick. A settled
    screen closes with an explicit finality line. A void or refund is NEUTRAL,
    never an error treatment.
    ⛔ "A redesign that gives loss its own expressive treatment for balance is
    reintroducing the defect." 99% of tickets lose, so this is the highest-traffic
    screen you will draw. No "so close". No re-entry inducement.

 6. THE YES/NO PAIR IS UNTOUCHABLE. YES = emerald hue 152 (--yes-*), NO = rose
    hue 22 (--no-*). Only inside betting actions and market-direction read-outs.
    Never navigation, never decoration, never re-hued, never inverted.
    ⚠️ SUCCESS IS NOT YES. App-state green (--success-*) is a DIFFERENT hue (166,
    chroma 0.12) from betting green (152, chroma 0.17), because a failed password
    reset was once painted in the losing-bet colour. Your slip has BOTH betting
    controls and app-state feedback — do not merge them.
    ⚠️ On a ten-row slip this is the biggest colour-dilution risk in the product:
    at most ONE lit control per row; unselected rows are neutral.

 7. COLOUR IS NEVER THE ONLY SIGNAL. Right/wrong, yes/no, win/loss are always
    paired with a word, arrow or glyph. ~8% of men are colour-blind, and here the
    colour means which way someone's money went.

 8. NO EMOJI. Anywhere, ever, in any language. Pictograms are stroke vectors or
    typographic marks. An emoji cannot be translated and renders unpredictably on
    cheap Android.

 9. MONEY IS MONO AND NEVER REFLOWS. Every amount: mono, tabular-nums, and
    NEVER letter-spaced — tracking is for identifiers, not money. Format is
    "TZS 1,234": code as prefix, one space, comma thousands, zero decimals.
    Negatives use U+2212, never a hyphen. The widest realistic figure is
    "TZS 1,000,000" — size the container for it so 999,999 → 1,000,000 does not
    shift layout. At a celebration the amount stays in the MONEY face; a display
    face for the big number was tried here and reversed.

10. THE TYPE SCALE IS CLOSED. Every size comes from a named step; a hand-typed
    size is a violation even when it looks right. Reading-copy floor is 12.5px —
    below that is a LABEL tier (uppercase, tracked, mono), forbidden for prose.
    ⚠️ The two micro tiers (9.5px, 8.5px) are ILLEGIBLE in Chinese. Do not put
    translatable copy in them.
    Every numeral that is DATA is mono + tabular — including numbers inside body
    sentences. Proportional digits make a changing figure twitch.

11. ACCESSIBILITY FLOORS. Text ≥ 4.5:1 measured on its ACTUAL composited surface;
    non-text and control boundaries ≥ 3.0:1. Tap targets ≥ 44px (the token floor
    is 40; 44 is preferred, and money controls are NEVER the exception — a stake
    chip shipped at 26px once). One focus recipe, visible everywhere, surviving
    forced-colors. ZERO horizontal overflow at 360px, in every language, with
    every overlay open.
    ⛔ WHEN A MONEY CONTROL FAILS CONTRAST, DARKEN THE FILL — NEVER LIGHTEN THE
    LABEL. The faintest ink step is an accessibility floor, not a taste setting;
    it already sits at 4.88:1 on the elevated surface, which is the tightest pair
    in the ramp.

12. EVERY REFUSAL STATES THE REASON AND THE NEXT STEP, and severity matches — a
    fixable problem is a warning, not a red error.

ONE THEME: deep royal indigo, hue 268. There is no light mode and there never
will be. Do not produce a light variant "for print". A second theme is a second
unproven contrast surface and every material rule here is derived from the
darkest one.

LANGUAGE — THE MEASURED BUDGET, NOT A GUESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Computed from the real 1,706-key dictionary, three locales, exact parity.

SWAHILI is the primary market and IS the sizing case. Design in Swahili first,
then check English.
  · Short labels (≤24 chars EN): median 1.167× · p90 1.741× · p95 2.000×
  · Button-sized (≤14 chars EN): median 1.182× · p90 1.889× · p95 2.250×
  · Prose (≥60 chars EN):        median 1.009× · p90 1.160×

  ⭐ THE BUDGET: fit short labels at 1.75×, PROVE them at 2.25×.
     Prose needs NO extra room — Swahili prose is the same length as English.
     Expansion here is a LABEL problem, not a paragraph problem. Spend the room
     where it is actually used.
  · 30% of control-sized labels exceed 1.35×. A control designed to 1.35× breaks
    on roughly one label in three.
  · Worst real label on the platform: "Top" (3 chars) → "Jedwali la Washindi"
    (19 chars) = ×6.33.
  · ⚠️ EVERY Swahili question opens with the fixed 4-character prefix "Je, ".
    Your ten question rows each carry it.

CHINESE — the sizing rule is the opposite of the intuition.
  · 62% shorter in CHARACTERS, but 88% of those characters are fullwidth and
    advance about twice a Latin one.
  · Modelled advance width: mean 0.715 · median 0.667 · p90 1.000.
  ⛔ DO NOT SHRINK ANY CONTAINER FOR CHINESE. At p90 it is exactly as wide as
     English.
  · No CJK webfont is downloaded, deliberately (Tanzanian mobile data). Chinese
    renders through system fallback and WILL NOT MATCH the Latin face you choose.
    Design for a face you do not control.

Money and timestamps are NEVER clipped, in any language. Labels wrap or
ellipsise; a truncated value stays reachable in full.

SET D — THREE TIER GLYPHS: Millionea, Supa, Mini.
  These extend the existing 178-glyph family and must be indistinguishable from

    · 24×24 viewBox, strokeWidth 1.9, round caps AND round joins
    · fill="none", stroke="currentColor" — no fills, no gradients, NO COLOUR
    · one coherent heraldic set
    · distinguishable from each other WITHOUT colour (law 7)
  ⚠️ These are drawn on a 24 grid but they RENDER at 9–18px, and the single most
  common size in the product is 14px. Prove legibility at 14px — that is the real

  Deliver each at 14px, 18px and 24px, plus the raw SVG path data.
  ⛔ Do not give them a colour, a glow, or a motion of their own. Identity motion

ART DIRECTION — the idea to design against
  Without gold, confetti, emoji or a climbing counter, a jackpot page risks
  reading flat. The answer is not decoration; it is this:

    THE SLIP IS A DOCUMENT, NOT A FORM.

  Ten numbered questions, mono figures, a countdown, a stamp when it locks, a
  published hash when it seals. That reads as an official entry into something
  real — rarer and more trustworthy than a casino page, and it is already the
  vocabulary this kit speaks: receipts, seals, mono money, resolution
  attestations. The house voice is heraldic and calm: a seal being pressed, not a
  jackpot ringing.
