# Visual audit — spacing, margins, type, alignment (2026-10-14/15)

**Status: RECORD of findings, not rules.** Every design rule this audit measured against lives in
[`docs/DESIGN_AUTHORITY.md`](../DESIGN_AUTHORITY.md) and its values in `src/app/globals.css`; nothing here restates one.
Ali (owner) asked on 2026-10-14 for "perfect spacing, perfect margins, perfect fonts, perfect everything", and on
2026-10-15 ruled that **responsiveness and UI consistency is a rule** — every fix below is verified at
320/360/390/768/1280/1920 × en/sw/zh and built from the kit only. Progress is recorded in `docs/VODACOM-PLAN.md` §0k;
this file only says what was found and who owns it.

## How it was read (read-only; nothing changed in the app)

- **Classic, production** (`www.50pick.tz`, signed out, GET only — non-GET requests aborted so no beacon fired):
  15 routes × 320/360/390/768/1280/1920 × sw/en/zh = 270 cells, 0 failed; each with `<html lang>` verified, a DOM probe,
  a fold shot and a full-page shot. Captured 2026-10-14 13:00–16:30 EAT (before `331048acb`, whose change is meant to
  move no classic pixel).
- **Journey, local** (in-memory dev server at `c14cb403e`, staff preview pass minted the [E2] way, seeded board and demo
  portfolio): 16 surfaces × 18 cells, plus 9 low-balance bet-sheet frames.
- Tap-target reach measured with `elementFromPoint` on production (320/360/1280 sw; 360/1280 en, zh).
- Seven readers read their sheets in full; three were cut off by the usage limit and their batches were covered by
  their crops, the probe data and the auditor's own 360/1280 sw reads.
- The captures, crops, probe JSON and the full per-finding records (`findings.jsonl`) are on OMEGA-COMPILE01 under
  `C:\50pick-ssd\visual-audit\` (not in the repo: ~1 GB of images).
- **Not measured:** classic signed-in pages on production (wallet, deposit/withdraw, profile, notifications,
  /positions, the bet and sell confirms), the admin console, WebKit/Firefox/real devices, menus and overlays not opened,
  empty/error states, large bet-sheet stakes, colour/contrast and hover/focus states.

Forgot-password meet the floor; How-to step numbers are mono; gold links in the hashed Terms and RG policy (§B4c), the
